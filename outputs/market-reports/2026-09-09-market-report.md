### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Two to three 30-minute strength sessions per week significantly improves running economy by increasing muscle-tendon stiffness — your legs become better "springs" generating more force per stride at the same energy cost. A 2026 randomised controlled trial (*Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners*, ResearchGate) confirmed gains persist even under fatigue, meaning you run stronger in late-race conditions. Schedule these on hard training days (speed or long run days) so recovery days stay truly easy — the principle of polarised training (80% Zone 1-2, 20% high-intensity) maximises adaptation while minimising overreach.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022's new **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR) compliance pathway** is now the most significant HVAC design shift in a generation — it evaluates the HVAC system as an integrated whole rather than rating individual components in isolation, allowing designers to use highly efficient chillers, fans, or controls to offset less-efficient units elsewhere in the system. California's Title 24-2025 (effective January 1, 2026) has already adopted 90.1-2022 as its baseline, and other jurisdictions are rolling it out through 2025-2026. Separately, the EPA's 2026 refrigerant rules have swept in sweeping changes to leak monitoring requirements and which refrigerants are permitted — if your projects are still specifying HFCs on the banned list, now is the time to audit specs and switch to approved low-GWP alternatives.
