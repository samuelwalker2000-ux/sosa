### 2. MY TAKE

The 10-year yield at 5.22% is the dominant regime variable right now. Crypto and risk equities cannot sustain a meaningful rally until yields peak and roll over — we are not there yet. BTC hovering at $83K on negative funding rates (first time in 3 months) tells you this is a squeezed, uncertain market, not a trending one. The ETF bid has turned into net outflows (~$448M YTD), and a hot CPI print tomorrow could easily flush BTC to the $79–80K range.

My stance: **defensive, patient**. I want to see CPI come in soft (≤3.3%) and the 10Y pull back below 5% before adding risk. Until then, I'm staying underweight crypto alts, avoiding duration in bonds, and watching the earnings prints for guidance. The only exception: if BTC holds $81K on bad CPI and doesn't flush, that's a potential asymmetric long with tight stops.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 study in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* (Zanini, Folland, Wu & Blagrove, 57(7):1546–1558) found that a 10-week supplementary strength programme improved running economy durability and high-intensity performance in fatigued states in well-trained male runners. Practically: 2–3 sessions per week of heavy lower-body lifts (squats, hip hinges) and hip/core stability work improves your late-race economy — the miles you run on tired legs. This is not a replacement for mileage; it's additive. Priority for runners logging 50km+/week who haven't incorporated resistance training.
