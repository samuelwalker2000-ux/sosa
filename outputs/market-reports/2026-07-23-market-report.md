### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** The American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) published its first resistance training guideline update in 17 years in 2026. Key takeaways: for maximal strength, train at ≥80% of your 1-rep max (1RM) for 2–3 sets per exercise; for power development, use 30–70% 1RM but emphasize moving the bar explosively during the concentric phase. ### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 commercial HVAC energy codes (driven by IECC 2024 and ASHRAE 90.1 updates) are mandating a 10–15% uplift in energy performance for new commercial buildings, with widespread heat pump integration now effectively required rather than optional. New minimum efficiency ratings (SEER2, EER2, COP) are significantly tighter than 90.1-2019 baselines, and the codes push hard on part-load efficiency and advanced controls — meaning static-pressure-reset VAV systems and demand-controlled ventilation are now baseline expectations, not upgrades.
