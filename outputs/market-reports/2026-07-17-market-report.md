### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** The American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) has published its landmark 2026 Resistance Training Guidelines — the first update in 17 years. Key updates include clearer progression protocols for both strength and hypertrophy goals, stronger evidence for training frequency (2–4 days/week for most adults), and new guidance integrating concurrent endurance + strength training. For runners specifically, supporting research confirms that heavy strength training improves running economy *durability* — meaning you maintain efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh — which is where races are actually won.
