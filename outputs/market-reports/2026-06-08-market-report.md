### 2. MY TAKE

This is not a garden-variety dip — it's a capitulation cascade. A Fear & Greed reading of 12 alongside a VIX that just jumped 40% in a session tells you forced selling, not just sentiment rotation, is doing the damage: $4.4B of spot BTC ETF outflows over 13 days, Saylor selling BTC for the first time since 2022, and a brutal cross-asset unwind (Nasdaq -4% on a single semiconductor air-pocket) all point to leverage getting flushed out simultaneously across crypto and tech equities. The dollar firming toward 100 and the 10Y pushing to 4.54% on hot jobs data is the real catalyst — markets are repricing for a Fed that may hike rather than cut, and that's poison for both long-duration tech multiples and speculative crypto.

My stance here is patient, leaning defensive — not aggressively buying the dip yet. Extreme Fear at 12 has historically marked capitulation lows (Dec 2018, Mar 2020, Jun 2022, Aug 2024), which argues for starting to build watchlists, but CPI on June 10 and the FOMC on June 16-17 are two live landmines in the next nine days that could easily push sentiment into single digits before it turns. I'd rather miss the first 10% of the bounce than catch a falling 10-year yield knife — sit close to cash/stables, keep dry powder for a confirmed turn after the Fed, and lean into the sectors already showing relative strength (Energy, Industrials, Staples) rather than chasing oversold beta.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A new randomized control trial in well-trained male runners found that adding heavy strength training and plyometrics to an endurance program measurably improved "running economy durability" — i.e., runners maintained efficient form and high-intensity output even in a fatigued state, not just at fresh, low-intensity paces. Practical takeaway: 2x/week heavy lower-body lifting (squats, deadlifts, calf raises) plus short plyo bursts (bounds, hops) is one of the highest-leverage additions you can make to a running block — it pays off specifically when races get hard late, which is when most runners fade.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 is now the live national baseline, and its Section 8 mandatory energy-monitoring requirement is the part designers should be pricing into projects today: any building over 25,000 sq ft must have an Energy Management Control System (EMCS) that tracks electrical energy by load category (HVAC, lighting, plug loads, etc.) at intervals no longer than 15 minutes, with a minimum 36-month data retention window. States including Alabama, Indiana, New Jersey, Oregon, West Virginia and DC have already certified adoption — if you're speccing controls/BAS scopes on mid-to-large commercial jobs in those jurisdictions (or anywhere expecting near-term adoption), build the sub-metering and trending architecture in now rather than retrofitting it post-occupancy.
