### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized trial in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* found that a 10-week supplementary strength program improved running economy durability and high-intensity performance in a *fatigued* state in trained male runners — not just at the start of a run. The key takeaway: heavy compound lifts (squats, Romanian deadlifts, hip thrusts) don't just make you efficient when fresh — they preserve form, power, and pace when fatigue accumulates in the late stages of a race. If you're training for anything longer than 10K, a structured off-season strength block is one of the highest ROI investments you can make per training hour.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Toronto's Board of Health formally requested the Province of Ontario adopt ASHRAE Standard 241 (*Control of Infectious Aerosols*) and Guideline 44-2024 into the Ontario Building Code, with ASHRAE's Society President writing directly to the Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing on August 19, 2026. Currently these standards are *voluntary* — meaning they carry no regulatory weight until adopted by an Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ). OSPE's submission to the same process underlines the enforcement gap: a ventilation standard only becomes a design requirement when mandated, not when published. Watch for OBC amendments in 2027 that could make ASHRAE 241 compliance mandatory for Part 3 occupancies — if you're spec-ing HVAC for institutional or multi-unit residential, building in 241-compliant filtration and ACH rates now avoids expensive retrofits later.
