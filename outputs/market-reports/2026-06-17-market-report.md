### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The A2L refrigerant transition is now a live code compliance issue, not a future one — the 2024 IBC/IRC/IFC/IMC already permit A2L refrigerants (e.g., R-454B, R-32) to replace high-GWP HFCs like R-410A, and jurisdictions like California (2025 CMC) now require A2L systems in human-comfort applications to comply with ASHRAE 15-2022, 15.2-2022, and 34-2022. Because A2Ls are mildly flammable, this triggers new mechanical, electrical, and fire-safety requirements for corridors, shafts, and multifamily/hotel occupancies — MEP teams should confirm with local AHJs now rather than at permit review.
