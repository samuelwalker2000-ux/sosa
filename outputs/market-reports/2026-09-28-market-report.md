### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 study in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* found that adding strength and plyometric training to a normal running program significantly preserved running economy under fatigue — economy deterioration dropped from 4.7% to 2.1% after 10 weeks, and the strength group improved time to exhaustion by 35% after a 90-minute run. The practical takeaway: if you're doing any endurance volume, lifting heavy (5-8 reps at 75-85% 1RM) two sessions per week is not just injury prevention — it's a direct performance multiplier for the last 30% of any long effort. Plyometrics (box jumps, bounding) amplify the neuromuscular adaptation further.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE published updated guidance in September 2026 for liquid cooling systems in high-density data centers, with a key finding: ASHRAE does not yet define "recommended" vs. "allowable" coolant supply temperature ranges for data center rack-level cooling, creating a gap that MEP engineers must fill through ASHRAE 90.4 and project-specific thermal analysis. This matters as AI GPU racks are now routinely exceeding 100 kW/rack — far beyond what traditional CRAC/CRAH air cooling can handle. Engineers specifying new data center facilities should be designing to CDU (Coolant Distribution Unit) systems and rear-door heat exchangers as the baseline, not the exception. Separately, ASHRAE and UNEP renewed their environmental partnership this month, reinforcing low-GWP refrigerant selection as a compliance-level requirement for new MEP system designs in most jurisdictions.
