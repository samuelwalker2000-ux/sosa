### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 BMJ-group study of ~147,000 people tracked over roughly 30 years found that 90–120 minutes of strength (resistance) training per week is the sweet spot for lowest mortality risk — with no additional longevity benefit from doing more than 120 minutes. The gains were amplified when strength work was combined with aerobic exercise. Practical takeaway: if you're running regularly, you only need 2 focused strength sessions (~45-60 min each) per week to capture essentially all the longevity upside — more isn't better, consistency is.
