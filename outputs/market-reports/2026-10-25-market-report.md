### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** California's 2025 Title 24 Building Codes took effect January 1, 2026, and include updated mechanical efficiency testing requirements alongside new NFPA 80, 90, 105, and 204 provisions for damper inspections. Most significantly for HVAC engineers, the A2L refrigerant transition is now in full effect: R-410A is out, with R-454B and R-32 as the primary replacements for residential and light commercial systems under the EPA's 700 GWP limit. Updated ASHRAE 15 and 34 editions add mandatory leak detection, ventilation, and labeling requirements for any system using A2L refrigerants — a compliance must-check before specifying new equipment on any 2026 project.
