### 2. MY TAKE

Equities and crypto have completely decoupled, and that's the real story right now. The S&P is grinding toward fresh highs above 7,500 and the Nasdaq just put in its best week since the April selloff, while Bitcoin is stuck in the low $60s — down roughly 30% from its 2026 highs near $90–100K — with Fear & Greed pinned at 19. This isn't broad risk-off; it's capital rotating out of speculative crypto and into mega-cap AI/chip names (the Intel-Apple reshoring deal is doing real work on the tape) while bonds and equities both hold up fine post-Fed. Kevin Warsh's hawkish debut plus Thursday's PCE print — expected to show inflation reaccelerating on energy costs — is the actual risk to both books, and equities look more exposed to a hot number than crypto, which has already priced in a lot of pain.

I'd stay patient on BTC and alts rather than catch the falling knife — the BTC-to-altcoin rotation has reportedly collapsed and alt season looks over for now, so there's no edge rotating down the cap table. Defensive on crypto until BTC reclaims and holds $66K; $60,800 support has held on the latest test but RSI/MACD are still bearish, so this reads as basing, not a confirmed reversal. On equities I'd stay long but tighten up — Accenture's -17% single-day move and Kroger's earnings miss are reminders this market is rewarding a very narrow set of winners (chips, AI capex) and punishing any miss hard. Thursday's PCE is the single biggest catalyst of the next two weeks for both books.
