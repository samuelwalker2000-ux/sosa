### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomized controlled trial (ResearchGate) found that strength training improves *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain metabolic efficiency under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. The key mechanism is neuromuscular adaptation that sustains stride mechanics at race pace after prolonged effort. Practical takeaway: prioritize heavy resistance, low-rep compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, single-leg work) twice a week to specifically target late-race economy — the gains show up most after the ~30 km mark in marathon-paced efforts.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 62.1-2022 introduced the Indoor Air Quality Procedure (IAQP) as an alternative compliance path for ventilation design — rather than prescriptive outdoor air rates by occupancy category, IAQP lets designers target specific contaminants of concern (CO₂, VOCs, particulates) and design the ventilation system around achieving acceptable concentrations of those pollutants. This is particularly useful for high-occupancy or mixed-use buildings where prescriptive rates would over-ventilate some zones and under-serve others. The 2026 trend in jurisdictions adopting ASHRAE 62.1 is increased audit and documentation requirements for IAQP submissions — engineers need air quality monitoring and modelling data to substantiate the design.
