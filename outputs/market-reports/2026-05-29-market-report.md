### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomised controlled trial confirmed that strength training improves *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain their efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. The practical takeaway: two dedicated sessions per week of 30–45 minutes is enough to see gains. Prioritise the four compound movements that cover 80% of runner needs: squat, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, and plank. Keep load manageable enough to run comfortably the next day — eccentric control (the slow lowering phase) is where most of the tendon-remodelling benefit comes from.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 state adoption deadline is now in effect — US states were required to certify adoption by early 2026 (two years after DOE's March 2024 affirmative determination). Key changes practitioners need to know: (1) Addendum aq increases on-site renewable energy capacity requirements from 0.5 W/sq ft to 0.75 W/sq ft for new commercial buildings; (2) a new optional *Mechanical System Performance Path* allows HVAC system efficiency tradeoffs using the Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR) metric, giving designers more flexibility versus the prescriptive path; (3) SEER2/HSPF2 metrics now apply to air-cooled heat pumps under 65,000 BTU/h capacity.
