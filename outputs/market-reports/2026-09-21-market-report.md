### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE TC 9.9's liquid cooling standards are dangerously behind the AI data centre buildout. As of 2026, TC 9.9 still lacks prescriptive thermal ranges for liquid cooling supply/return temperatures, coolant chemistry requirements, or minimum flow rates per unit of heat load — gaps that were tolerable in the air-cooling era but are now critical: every AI GPU deployment at scale (GB200, Blackwell) runs liquid-cooled. TC 9.9 working groups have accelerated following the GB200 announcement, with a comprehensive liquid cooling revision expected within 12–24 months, but MEP engineers specifying facilities today must rely on OEM specs (NVIDIA, Dell) and engineering judgement rather than ASHRAE tables. Practical implication: design liquid loops to W3/W4 supply temperature classes (enabling dry-cooler/cooling-tower rejection year-round) and document the engineering basis explicitly, since permit reviewers increasingly ask for the ASHRAE reference that doesn't yet exist.
