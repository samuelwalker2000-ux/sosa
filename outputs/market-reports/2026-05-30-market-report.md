### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomised controlled trial (*ResearchGate*) found that strength training improves **running economy durability** — meaning trained runners maintain their efficiency under fatigue rather than just when fresh. The mechanism is neuromuscular: heavier lifts (squats, RDLs) improve force production and stiffness of the Achilles tendon-calf complex, reducing the energy cost of each stride in the final kilometres of a race. If you're training for a 10K or longer, add 2 heavy lower-body sessions per week (3–4 sets, 4–6 reps at 80–85% 1RM) — this is the single highest-leverage addition a runner can make to their training.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2025 introduced the **Mechanical System Performance Rating (MSPR)** method as a new compliance pathway for commercial buildings, detailed in Appendix L. Rather than prescriptive HVAC equipment criteria, MSPR uses a **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR)** — comparing the proposed design's total energy efficiency against a defined baseline HVAC system. This is significant for MEP engineers because it allows more flexibility to justify high-performance systems (e.g., VRF, heat pump arrays, high-efficiency chillers) on a whole-system basis rather than individual equipment specs. Critically, **New York City adopted 2025 ASHRAE 90.1 from March 30, 2026** — any NYC project permitted from that date must comply with the new standard, including the MSPR path.
