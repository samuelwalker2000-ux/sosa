### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (ResearchGate, *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*) found that strength training improves **running economy durability** — meaning trained runners maintain efficiency *under fatigue*, not just when fresh. The key mechanism: eccentric loading (e.g. slow Nordic curls, single-leg RDLs) remodels tendon tissue and restores load tolerance, which pays off in the final 10K of a marathon. If you run more than 40 miles/week and don't lift, this is the single highest-ROI addition to your training plan.
