### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric loading — specifically slow, controlled lowering under weight — is one of the most underutilised tools for runners. A 2025 study (Applied Sciences, Jan 2025) found that a 20-week endurance + eccentric strength program improved running economy by 4% and VO2max by 4.6%. The mechanism: eccentric contractions remodel tendon tissue and restore load tolerance, directly cutting injury risk in high-mileage runners. Practical tip — add 3×10 slow Nordic curls and single-leg Romanian deadlifts (3-second eccentric) twice a week; these don't bulk you up but will noticeably improve stride efficiency within 6–8 weeks.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** As of January 1, 2026, the EPA's Technology Transitions Rule prohibits the manufacture of new residential and light commercial HVAC equipment using refrigerants with a GWP above 700 — effectively phasing out R-410A (GWP ~2,088) from new production. The primary A2L replacements are R-454B (GWP ~466, classified under ASHRAE Standard 34-2024 as A2L) and R-32 (GWP ~675). Practical implications for MEP engineers: equipment schedules and specs must now reference A2L-compliant units; machine room sizing under ASHRAE 15-2024 may require larger minimum volumes for A2L flammability provisions; and technician certification for A2L handling (leak detection protocols, storage limits) is now a job-site compliance requirement. If you're still specifying R-410A equipment, verify whether it's existing inventory or a new-manufacture unit — the line matters.
