### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2025 introduces new annualised efficiency metrics for heat pump systems: IVEC (cooling annualised), IVHE (heating annualised, warmer climates), and IVHEC (heating annualised, cold climates). These replace the older single-point SEER/HSPF-based ratings for prescriptive compliance and energy credits, aligning 90.1 with the latest federal test procedures.
