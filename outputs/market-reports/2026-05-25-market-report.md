### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomised controlled trial (PMC12885319) on high time-under-tension (TUT) strength training found that using a slow eccentric phase (3-4 seconds) on compound lifts modulates neuromuscular fatigue without spiking post-session fatigue markers — making it compatible with same-week endurance sessions. For runners, this means heavy slow-eccentrics (Romanian deadlifts, split squats, calf raises) can be slotted 24-48h before an easy run without performance compromise. The take: TUT training delivers running economy gains via improved tendon stiffness and neuromuscular efficiency, rather than muscle hypertrophy — you get faster and more efficient without getting heavier.
