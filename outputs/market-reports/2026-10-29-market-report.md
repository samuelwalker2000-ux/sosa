Position defensively: trim growth/tech exposure, avoid chasing crypto until BTC decisively reclaims $83K+, and keep dry powder for the November jobs print (Nov 6) and CPI (Nov 10) which will determine whether the December hike is a coin flip or a lock.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 edition of NFPA 10 (Standard for Portable Fire Extinguishers) introduces a significant change allowing fire extinguisher inspections to be performed using remote-monitoring technology for the first time. This modernises the inspection regime — instead of requiring a physical technician visit for each inspection interval, smart sensors and IoT-connected devices can now satisfy the code requirement, reducing labour costs and improving compliance visibility on large campuses, commercial buildings, and industrial facilities. MEP engineers and facility managers designing or auditing fire protection systems should verify that any remote-monitoring platform proposed for compliance is listed and evaluated to meet the 2026 NFPA 10 requirements before specifying it.
