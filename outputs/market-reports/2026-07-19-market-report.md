### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Research consistently shows that sequencing strength before endurance in the same training session minimizes the "interference effect" — the phenomenon where concurrent training blunts strength gains. A recent 20-week study on trained runners found that combined strength + endurance programs improved running economy by 4% and VO2max by 4.6%, with the greatest gains coming after 24+ sessions. If you're a runner adding lifting: keep strength blocks short (30-40 min, heavy compound lifts), do them first, and give 6+ hours between sessions when possible to maximize adaptation without compromise. Source: MDPI Applied Sciences (2025); Frontiers in Sports & Active Living (2025).

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The A2L refrigerant transition is no longer optional — virtually all new commercial HVAC installations in 2026 must use A2L refrigerants such as R-32 or R-454B under the latest ASHRAE 90.1-2025 and IECC 2024 frameworks. A2Ls are "mildly flammable" (classified per ISO 817), which triggers mandatory compliance with ASHRAE 15-2022 and UL 60335-2-40: these standards govern refrigerant charge limits, mandatory mechanical room ventilation, and refrigerant leak detection systems. For MEP engineers, this means specifying leak detection sensors and ventilation tie-ins at the design stage — not as an afterthought — and coordinating with AHJs early since many jurisdictions are still updating their inspection checklists for A2L systems. Source: ManualJ Pro (2026 HVAC Energy Codes); Nautilus Data Technologies (ASHRAE Liquid Cooling, 2026).
