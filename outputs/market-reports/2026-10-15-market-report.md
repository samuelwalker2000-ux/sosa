### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial (Zanini, Folland, Wu & Blagrove; *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, 57(7):1546–1558) found that a 10-week supplementary strength training program significantly improved running economy throughout 90 minutes of heavy-intensity running. The key mechanism is neuromuscular efficiency — the body learns to recruit motor units more economically, reducing the energy cost per stride. Practically: 2 lower-body strength sessions per week (squats, single-leg work, Nordic hamstrings) for 8–12 weeks is the minimum effective dose to see measurable improvements in running economy.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE has opened Addendum d to ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 62.1-2025 (*Ventilation and Acceptable Indoor Air Quality*) for a 30-day public review running October 9 to November 8, 2026. The addendum proposes mandatory duct leakage testing requirements, which would establish an objective, measurable benchmark for whether a duct system meets the designer's stated performance expectations — introducing accountability into construction that currently relies almost entirely on workmanship. For MEP consultants, this is worth tracking: if adopted, specifications and commissioning scopes for air-handling systems would need to include testing protocols and acceptance criteria aligned with the new requirement.
