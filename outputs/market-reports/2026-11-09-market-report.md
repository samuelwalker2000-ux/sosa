The 10Y yield sitting at 4.86% and a DXY approaching 103 means the macro headwind is still present. Positioning: **defensive and patient**. Hold cash, reduce leverage, and wait for the CPI tape before making a directional call in either crypto or equities.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* found that well-trained male runners who added a 10-week supplementary strength program maintained their high-intensity running performance significantly longer than a running-only control group, while the control group declined. The practical takeaway: 2–3 sessions per week of heavy lower-body lifting (squats, Romanian deadlifts, heavy calf raises) is sufficient to improve running economy durability without compromising aerobic training — the key is keeping the total weekly mileage constant. Heavy calf raises in particular are repeatedly highlighted for their direct link to running economy and Achilles tendon stiffness.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE has opened a 30-day public review (Oct 23 – Nov 22, 2026) for proposed changes to Standard 62.2, the ventilation standard for low-rise residential buildings, including a virtual interim meeting of the SSPC 62.2 Envelope Subcommittee on Nov 18. Separately, ASHRAE released a letter to the California Energy Commission (CEC) responding to the pre-rulemaking draft for California's 2028 Title 24 Energy Code — ASHRAE noted that multiple ASHRAE standards (including 90.1 and 62.1) are referenced in the California Energy Code, and urged the CEC to maintain alignment with current published editions rather than creating California-specific divergence. For MEP engineers in California, this is worth watching: Title 24 2028 may tighten HVAC minimum efficiency requirements and ventilation rates beyond current ASHRAE 90.1-2022 baselines.
