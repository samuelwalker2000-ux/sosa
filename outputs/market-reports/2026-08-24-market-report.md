**Today's insight:** A long-term study tracking 147,000 people over 30 years (published June 2026, ScienceDaily) found that just 90–120 minutes of strength training per week delivers some of the largest longevity gains of any exercise modality — more than doubling the duration didn't improve outcomes proportionately. ### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NFPA 70) introduces a critical change: arc-flash hazard labeling is now required on **all** service equipment regardless of amperage — not just systems above a certain threshold as in prior editions. For MEP engineers and commissioning teams, this means every distribution board, MCC, and switchgear assembly on a project must have compliant incident energy labels applied before energisation or final inspection. This closes a long-standing gap where smaller 100A and 200A panels often shipped without arc-flash analysis. Coordinate with the electrical engineer of record early to ensure short-circuit and coordination studies are scoped to include all service equipment from the design phase.
