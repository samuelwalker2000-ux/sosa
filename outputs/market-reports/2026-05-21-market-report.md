### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 meta-analysis (PMC) confirms that heavy resistance training at ≥90% of 1RM for 10–14 weeks produces greater improvements in running economy and time trial performance than plyometric training over equivalent periods. The mechanism is neuromuscular — maximal loads recruit more motor units and stiffen the musculotendinous system, improving force transmission per stride. Practical takeaway: if you're a runner trying to improve economy, prioritize heavy compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, hip thrusts) at near-maximal intensity 2x/week over jump training.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** States were required to certify adoption of ASHRAE 90.1-2022 by early 2026, making the new Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR) compliance pathway in Appendix L a live obligation for many jurisdictions. TSPR evaluates the entire HVAC system as an integrated whole rather than rating individual components in isolation — a significant shift that rewards VRF systems, heat recovery chillers, and advanced controls that optimise part-load performance. Separately, new EPA rules effective in 2026 tighten leak monitoring requirements and restrict which refrigerants can be installed, directly affecting retrofit and new-build chiller/DX specifications — engineers should verify equipment selections against the updated allowable refrigerant list before issuing for-construction drawings.
