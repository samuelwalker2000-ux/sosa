### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric strength training is one of the most underutilised tools for runners — the slow, controlled lengthening of a muscle under load (e.g. Nordic curls, slow-tempo calf drops, Romanian deadlifts with 4-second lowering phase) remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, and significantly reduces injury risk in the Achilles, hamstrings, and quads. A 2025 RCT in *Sports Medicine* found that 8 weeks of eccentric loading improved running economy durability in well-trained runners by reducing late-race mechanical breakdown. Two sessions per week during a build phase is sufficient — prioritise it over additional easy mileage if you're injury-prone.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE's 2026 Annual Conference in Austin highlighted ground-source heat pumps (GSHPs) as the central technology for decarbonising commercial buildings — particularly attractive for MEP engineers in jurisdictions phasing out gas boilers. Recent ASHRAE standard updates (aligned with ASHRAE 90.1-2025 and 62.1) raise filtration, fan efficiency, and IAQ documentation requirements, meaning every new project now carries tighter design parameters: higher MERV ratings for air handling units, minimum fan energy indices (FEI), and documented indoor air quality compliance paths. For MEP engineers in Ontario, these align closely with the direction of travel in OBC revisions expected in the 2026–2027 cycle — now is a good time to check design standards on current projects against the updated ASHRAE thresholds and any applicable CSA updates.
