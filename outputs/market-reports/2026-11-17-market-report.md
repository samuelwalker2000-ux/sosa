### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Combining heavy-load strength training with plyometrics is the most effective approach for improving running economy — multiple 2026 meta-analyses confirm this. The mechanism is neuromuscular: heavy loads increase motor unit recruitment and stiffness of the musculotendinous unit, while plyometrics improve the stretch-shortening cycle. Practically, two sessions per week of squats/deadlifts (3–5 reps @80–85% 1RM) paired with box jumps or bounding integrated on easy run days gives the strongest return for endurance runners. (Sources: PMC11052887, PMC11258194)

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The temporary GFCI exception for listed HVAC equipment under NEC 2026 expired on September 1, 2026 — GFCI protection is now required for all HVAC equipment in applicable locations with no carve-out for listed units. This catches some engineers off guard who assumed the exception would be extended. Simultaneously, ASHRAE 15-2024 and 34-2024 are driving new A2L refrigerant requirements (R-32, R-454B, R-466A) for system design: leak detection must be provided for systems exceeding the refrigerant quantity limits, with new ventilation, pipe labeling, and component requirements. Projects designed or permitted from early 2026 onward should already be incorporating these; anything older in construction should be reviewed for compliance before AHJ inspection.
