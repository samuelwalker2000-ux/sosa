### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** The American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) published its landmark 2026 Resistance Training Guidelines this month — the first major update in 17 years. The key practical shift: the guidelines now formally endorse a broader rep range (5–30 reps per set) for hypertrophy and strength adaptations, replacing the older "8–12 rep" dogma. For runners specifically, the guidelines highlight that even 2 strength sessions per week at moderate-to-heavy loads (70–85% 1RM) significantly improve running economy and reduce overuse injury risk — reinforcing the case for lifting as a non-negotiable part of endurance training.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The refrigerant transition away from R-410A is hitting critical mass in 2026. Effective January 1, 2025 (with manufacturer sell-through into 2026), the EPA's AIM Act prohibits new equipment using R-410A (GWP 2,088) in split-system residential and light-commercial applications. The replacement A2L refrigerants — primarily R-32 (GWP 675) and R-454B (GWP 466) — are mildly flammable (ASHRAE Class A2L), requiring updated installation practices, detector requirements under NFPA 70/ASHRAE 15, and new equipment certifications. Engineers specifying HVAC systems for projects currently in design should confirm equipment manufacturers' availability timelines for A2L-compatible systems, as supply chains are still catching up — particularly for large-tonnage VRF systems.
