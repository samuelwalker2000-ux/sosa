### 2. MY TAKE

Bitcoin just logged its seventh straight weekly outflow from spot ETFs — the longest streak since the products launched — with IBIT alone shedding $444.5M in a single Friday session and $1.3B for the week. That's not noise; that's institutional money walking out the door while Fear & Greed sits at 17. Equities are holding up far better than crypto right now, which tells you this is a crypto-specific deleveraging, not a broad risk-off move — Micron just posted a blowout quarter and chip demand for AI is still real, even as Apple and Microsoft eat margin headlines on price hikes.

My stance here is patient, not defensive. BTC under $60K with extreme fear and a seven-day IBIT outflow streak is a classic capitulation setup, not a reason to chase the downside. I wouldn't add aggressively until outflows actually stabilize — watch for IBIT to post a green day — but this is not the moment to panic-sell either. On the equity side, the chip-stock rotation into defensives looks tactical and earnings-driven (Micron strength vs. Apple/Microsoft cost pass-through), not a regime change; I'd stay invested through the holiday week and let the jobs report Thursday set the next tone.
