### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 is now the current national reference edition, and states have a certification deadline of early 2026 to confirm adoption (several, including NJ, OR, and WV, already have). The big practical change for commercial buildings over 25,000 sq ft is the new Section 8 mandate: an Energy Management Control System that submeters electrical loads by category (HVAC, interior/exterior lighting, plug loads, process loads) at 15-minute intervals minimum, with 36 months of data retention — a real submetering/BMS scope-of-work item for any new commercial design from here on.
