### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* found that adding strength training to a runner's programme did not improve running economy when fresh — but significantly reduced economy decline under fatigue. After 10 weeks, the strength group's economy degraded only 2.1% over a 90-minute run versus 4.7% in controls. The practical upshot: strength work doesn't make you faster at the start, it keeps you efficient at the end. Prioritise it for marathon/half-marathon prep and long interval sessions.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE released a public review addendum to Guideline 36 (High-Performance Sequences of Operation for HVAC Systems) adding standardised sequences for two common hot water and chilled water plant designs. Guideline 36 is the industry benchmark for optimised HVAC control logic — adding chilled/hot water plant SOOs brings more complex central plant systems into the same consistent framework already established for air-handling units and VAV boxes. Engineers specifying BAS sequences should review and incorporate these additions, as they're likely to be referenced by owners and commissioning agents in next-year project specs.
