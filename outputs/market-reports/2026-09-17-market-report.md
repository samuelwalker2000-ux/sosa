### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** California Title 24 (2025 energy code cycle) took effect January 1, 2026, with explicit alignment to ASHRAE Guideline 36 control sequences for air-handling units and terminal units. This raises the bar for BAS (Building Automation System) commissioning documentation significantly — projects in California now require compliant Guideline 36 programming libraries, verified sequence testing, and functional performance testing records at occupancy.
