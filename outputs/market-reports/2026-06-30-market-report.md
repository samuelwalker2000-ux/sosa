### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Research consistently shows that 2 strength sessions per week — placed on easy run days, separated from quality workouts by at least 6 hours — significantly improves *running economy durability*: the ability to maintain efficient form and pace under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. The minimum effective dose for runners is simple: squat, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, and plank — these four exercises cover ~80% of what a runner needs. The key finding from a 2025 randomized control trial: strength-trained runners maintained better high-intensity performance in the *later miles* of a race, where the untrained group degraded. If you're only doing one thing in the gym, make it 2×/week lower-body strength on your easy days.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 ASHRAE Annual Conference (Austin, TX, June 27) launched significant development of **ASHRAE Standard 240P** — *Evaluating Greenhouse Gas (GHG) and Carbon Emissions in Building Design, Construction and Operation* — which establishes a standardized methodology for calculating whole-life carbon across building systems, including embodied carbon in MEP equipment. For MEP engineers, this standard matters because it will increasingly be referenced by municipal codes and institutional clients as a compliance tool for decarbonization targets.
