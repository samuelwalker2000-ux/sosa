Meanwhile crypto's Fear & Greed reading of 12 is about as fearful as it gets, despite BTC holding $64K and up 6% on the week with dominance still climbing toward 59%. I'd stay patient into the FOMC decision Wednesday rather than chase either side. Equities have run on a soft narrative (Iran MOU, falling oil, falling VIX) that could reverse fast if the Fed leans hawkish on language even without a rate change — and a 10Y yield ticking back up toward 4.5%+ is a yellow flag for that risk. Patient, not defensive: let the FOMC print before committing fresh capital either direction.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A recent systematic review/meta-analysis (PMC11052887) found that strength training — particularly heavy-load and plyometric methods — meaningfully improves running economy in middle- and long-distance runners, with one related RCT showing it also improves "running economy durability" (i.e., efficiency holds up better late in fatigued efforts). Practical takeaway: 1-2 heavy-load or plyometric strength sessions per week alongside running volume can pay off most in the back half of races.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** As of January 1, 2026, Ontario's transition period to the 2024 Ontario Building Code fully ended, with the updated code now explicitly tying HVAC ventilation requirements to ASHRAE 62.1 (Ventilation for Acceptable Indoor Air Quality) — a harmonization push with the National Building Code. Separately, ASHRAE 90.1-2022 (now the baseline national edition) mandates Energy Management Control Systems for buildings over 25,000 sq ft, monitoring electrical energy by load category at minimum 15-minute intervals — worth checking new commercial projects against both requirements now that the grace period has lapsed.
