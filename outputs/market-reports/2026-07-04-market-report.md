### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** The American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) released its landmark 2026 Resistance Training Position Stand in March — the first comprehensive update in 17 years. The headline finding is deceptively simple: *any* resistance training modality (elastic bands, bodyweight, machines, free weights) produces meaningful improvements in strength, hypertrophy, and physical function, with the key variable being consistency, not equipment sophistication. The update also re-emphasises eccentric loading — the slow, controlled lengthening phase of a lift — as one of the highest-ROI tools for tendon remodelling and injury prevention, particularly relevant for runners stacking strength work alongside volume blocks.

*Source: ACSM, "Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults," 2026*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Liquid cooling adoption in data centers has reached approximately 37% in 2026 — up from just 3% in 2021 — driven entirely by the thermal demands of AI GPU clusters. Single-phase immersion cooling (servers submerged in dielectric fluid) is now in production at hyperscalers including Meta and Microsoft, delivering 40% reductions in cooling energy cost and 90% less water consumption versus traditional air-cooled CRAC/CRAH systems. MEP engineers designing these facilities must rethink secondary cooling loops, containment strategies, fluid management, and fire suppression interfaces — ASHRAE 90.4 (Data Center Energy Efficiency) and NFPA 75 (Fire Protection of IT Equipment) are the governing standards, but both are still catching up to full-immersion deployments. Trane Technologies' acquisition of LiquidStack (February 2026) signals that traditional HVAC OEMs are pivoting hard into this space.
