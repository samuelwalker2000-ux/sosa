### 2. MY TAKE

The interesting tell today isn't the price action, it's the gap between price and sentiment. BTC is back above $63,600, ETF flows just flipped positive for the first time since the brutal mid-June outflow streak (IBIT-led, ~$86M net inflow), and SOL/XRP are both ripping 2–5% — yet Fear & Greed is still pinned at 14, Extreme Fear. That combination — price recovering, flows inflecting, sentiment still terrible — is usually where the asymmetric trade sits, not where you stay defensive. Equities are coasting on Thursday's chip-driven, Iran-deal-driven rally into a dead weekend with zero new information until Monday.

My stance: lean offensive on crypto specifically. The $3.4B ETF bleed earlier in June looks increasingly cyclical rather than structural, and a sentiment indicator this depressed with price already turning is a classic early-recovery signature, not a falling knife. On equities I stay constructive but patient — the semis/onshoring trade (Marvell, Sandisk, Intel) is a real multi-quarter structural story worth holding through the Apple-Intel chip partnership headlines, but I wouldn't chase the index into Monday without seeing whether the Iran-deal rally has legs once a fresh trading session actually opens.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** New research from Virginia Commonwealth University and Harvard Medical School on nearly 1,000 Boston Marathon runners found that adding one quality session per week (not just more mileage) improved finish times by about 16.2 minutes on average, and that smart tapering plus fewer-but-better weekly runs outperformed simply piling on volume. ### 10. MEP ENGINEERING
