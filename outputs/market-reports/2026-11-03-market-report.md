### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial (Zanini, Folland, Wu & Blagrove, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*) found that a 10-week supplementary strength program significantly improved running economy durability and fatigued high-intensity performance in well-trained male runners. The practical takeaway: 2–3 short lower-body strength sessions per week — squats, lunges, single-leg work — run alongside your regular mileage, not instead of it. The gains compound over 8–10 weeks, so consistent execution beats intensity on any individual session.
