### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** **NEC 2026 — New GFCI Requirements for Outdoor HVAC Equipment (Effective September 1, 2026).** The 2026 National Electrical Code now mandates that all outdoor HVAC equipment be protected by one of four approved GFCI configurations. The change was driven by chronic nuisance-tripping of conventional GFCIs when paired with variable-speed drives (VSDs), which are now ubiquitous in modern HVAC systems. The solution is equipment-specific GFCI devices or isolation transformers that can distinguish true ground faults from the high-frequency leakage currents produced by VSD switching — a critical consideration for any new mechanical design or retrofit drawing set.
