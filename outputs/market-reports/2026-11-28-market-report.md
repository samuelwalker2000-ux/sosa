### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy resistance training and plyometric work (2–3 sessions/week) are the most evidence-backed modalities for improving running economy in endurance athletes, per a 2018 Sports Medicine systematic review. The mechanism is neuromuscular — specifically improving rate of force development so each foot strike wastes less energy. The practical takeaway: if you're running 4+ days a week, replace one easy run with a session of heavy squats/deadlifts plus 3–4 sets of bounding or box jumps. Don't skip it thinking more miles will cover it — research says they won't for most trained runners.
