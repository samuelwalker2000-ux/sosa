### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Recent 2024–25 research confirms that strength training improves *running economy durability* — i.e., athletes maintain efficient biomechanics under fatigue, not just when fresh. The mechanism: heavy resistance training (3–5 sets of 3–6 reps at 80–90% 1RM) increases tendon stiffness and neuromuscular recruitment, meaning less energy is wasted at each foot strike in the late miles of a run. Practical application: two sessions of compound lower-body lifts (Romanian deadlifts, single-leg press, Bulgarian split squats) per week is enough to see economy gains within 8–10 weeks — keep reps low and weight high to maximise the tendon adaptation without building unnecessary muscle mass.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The A2L refrigerant transition is now effectively complete for new residential and light commercial HVAC equipment. R-454B (sold as Opteon XP44 by Chemours and Puron Advance by Carrier, GWP 466) and R-32 (GWP 675) have replaced R-410A (GWP 2,088) across major OEM product lines. For MEP engineers, the key compliance implications are immediate: A2L refrigerants are mildly flammable (ASHRAE Class A2L), requiring refrigerant detectors and ventilation safeguards per ASHRAE 15-2022 and the 2024 International Mechanical Code in spaces where charge limits could be exceeded — equipment rooms and confined plant rooms need design review for projects currently in documentation. Technician re-certification and updated leak detection protocols are no longer optional.
