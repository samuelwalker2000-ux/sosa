Crypto is the tell. Fear & Greed at 27 while equities rip to ATHs means this isn't a risk-on rotation — it's equity-specific relief driven by geopolitics and MSFT earnings. If CPI comes in hot, the September hike becomes consensus and both bonds and crypto get another leg down.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial found that 20 weeks of integrated strength training improved running economy by ~4% (oxygen cost) and increased VO2max by 4.6% in well-trained male runners. The mechanism is eccentric muscle-tendon adaptation — slow, controlled lowering phases during squats and single-leg work remodel tendon tissue and reduce ground contact time. Practical takeaway: 2–3 sessions/week at 50–80% 1RM with a focus on eccentric tempo (3–4 seconds down) is the minimum effective dose for significant performance gains without compromising run training.
