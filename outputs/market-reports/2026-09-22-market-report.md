### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Recent (June 2026) research published on ScienceDaily identified the optimal strength training "sweet spot" for longevity: 2–3 resistance sessions per week with compound lifts showed the greatest survival benefit, while more frequent training showed diminishing returns. For runners, this translates directly — systematic reviews confirm that heavy or explosive strength training alongside endurance work improves running economy (efficiency under fatigue) without requiring high volume. Two quality strength sessions per week is enough to move the needle; the sessions should prioritise compound movements (squats, deadlifts, single-leg work) over isolation exercises.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NFPA 70) took effect September 1, 2026 in adopting jurisdictions, introducing a critical HVAC change: the previous Exception No. 2 exemption for HVAC equipment from GFCI protection has expired, and HVAC units must now be protected by a newly listed Class C Special Purpose Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter (SPGFCI). This matters practically for MEP engineers on new commercial and residential projects — rooftop and mechanical room HVAC equipment that was previously exempt now requires a compliant SPGFCI device in the circuit design. Verify jurisdiction adoption before applying, but major provinces and states are moving quickly to adopt the 2026 NEC.
