### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 meta-analysis of five studies covering 894 competitive distance runners found that adding strength training to an endurance program improves running economy by an average of 4.6% (range: 3–8.1%), without changing VO2 max. The mechanism is neuromuscular — explosive and heavy-load resistance training stiffens tendons and improves force transmission, so each stride costs less oxygen. The practical takeaway: 2–3 sessions per week of heavy lifts (squats, deadlifts) or plyometrics added to your existing mileage will make you faster without needing to run more.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The **NEC 2026** now mandates GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) protection for **all outdoor HVAC equipment**, effective September 1, 2026. This extends existing GFCI requirements to condensing units, air handlers, and other outdoor mechanical equipment previously exempt in many jurisdictions. For MEP engineers, this means specifying GFCI-protected receptacles or breakers for all outdoor mechanical equipment on new projects — and flagging this as a change-order risk on projects currently in construction that adopted NEC 2023 or earlier. Confirm with the AHJ on permit cutoff dates to determine which edition governs each project.
