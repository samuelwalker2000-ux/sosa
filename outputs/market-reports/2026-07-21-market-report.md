### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** ACSM released its first update to Resistance Training Guidelines in 17 years in 2026, now formally endorsing eccentric and isometric protocols for performance athletes. The key finding: eccentric loading (slow, controlled lengthening under load) is among the most effective tools for both injury prevention and running economy improvement — it remodels tendon tissue and restores load tolerance in ways concentric work can't replicate. If you're running and lifting, prioritise eccentric squats, Nordic hamstring curls, and slow-tempo calf raises over high-rep concentric sets for maximum transfer.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 commercial HVAC energy codes (driven by IECC 2024 and updated ASHRAE 90.1) are now mandating the transition from R-410A to A2L refrigerants — specifically R-32 and R-454B — in new commercial equipment. This is not a future concern: equipment availability is shifting now, and the design implications are real. A2L refrigerants are mildly flammable (lower flammability limit applies), which means NFPA 1 and refrigerant safety requirements are being updated for occupied spaces, mechanical room clearances, and leak detection requirements. Engineers specifying or reviewing HVAC equipment in 2026 should confirm A2L compliance and flag if older R-410A specs are still in drawings — it's a RFI risk and a code non-conformance issue on current projects.
