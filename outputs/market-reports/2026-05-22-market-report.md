### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2024–2025 randomised controlled trial confirmed that strength training improves *running economy durability* — meaning athletes maintain mechanical efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. Two focused sessions per week (30–45 min each) built around squat, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, and plank covers approximately 80% of the strength benefit a runner needs. Pick weights that are challenging but allow comfortable running the next day; the goal is neuromuscular adaptation, not bodybuilder hypertrophy.

*Source: ResearchGate — "Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners: A Randomized Control Trial"*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 introduces the **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR)** pathway in Appendix L — a significant shift in how HVAC system efficiency is evaluated. Rather than assessing individual components in isolation, TSPR measures the integrated performance of the entire HVAC system, allowing designers to offset a less-efficient terminal unit with a highly efficient chiller plant, for example. Several states (including New Jersey, Oregon, and Washington DC) have already adopted 90.1-2022 as of early 2026, making TSPR compliance a live design consideration for ongoing projects — particularly in healthcare facilities and data centres where HVAC load profiles are complex.

*Source: Envigilance — ASHRAE 90.1 Compliance Guide 2026*
