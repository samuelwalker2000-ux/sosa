### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (published in *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*) found that strength training improves **running economy durability** — not just fresh-state efficiency but the ability to maintain that efficiency under fatigue late in a race. The key mechanism: moderate-to-heavy loads (50–80% 1RM) build propulsive force capacity, so the neuromuscular system degrades more slowly. Practically, 2 sessions per week of compound lower-body work (squats, Romanian deadlifts, single-leg press) is sufficient stimulus for measurable gains in a 12-week block.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The **2026 National Electrical Code (NFPA 70)** becomes effective today — September 1, 2026. A significant new requirement affects HVAC design directly: all single- and multi-family residential buildings must now provide **GFCI protection for all 208V and 240V single-phase HVAC equipment**. This is a departure from previous editions where GFCI was typically limited to lower-voltage circuits. MEP engineers and electrical designers specifying residential systems — particularly mini-splits, heat pumps, and packaged terminal air conditioners (PTACs) — must verify that panel schedules and protective device selections comply. GFCI breakers at the panel are the standard solution; coordinate with the equipment manufacturer on nuisance-tripping tolerance for variable-frequency drives and soft-start units.
