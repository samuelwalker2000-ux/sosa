### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial published via ResearchGate found that strength training not only improves baseline running economy but specifically improves *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintained their metabolic efficiency deeper into fatigued states and produced better high-intensity performance at the end of races. Two well-programmed sessions per week (30–45 min each) is sufficient: prioritize squat, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, and plank as the core four. Pick loads that feel challenging but leave you able to run the following day — the adaptation is cumulative, not acute.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 introduces the **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR)** pathway in Appendix L — a fundamental shift from prescriptive minimum-efficiency requirements for individual components to evaluating how efficiently the *entire HVAC system* performs as an integrated whole. States were required to certify adoption by early 2026. For mechanical engineers, this means design teams can now justify systems that sacrifice a component-level efficiency metric if the overall system outperforms — enabling more creative integrated solutions (e.g., active chilled beams paired with lower-efficiency chillers that still beat the prescriptive baseline on TSPR). Pair this with the updated demand control ventilation requirements now tied to climate zone and ASHRAE 62.1 airflow rates, and 90.1-2022 demands a system-level design mindset from the first schematic.
