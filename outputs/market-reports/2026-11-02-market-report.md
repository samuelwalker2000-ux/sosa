### 2. MY TAKE

This is a fear-and-opportunity moment, not a systemic meltdown. The Friday sell-off reads as a crowded-tech unwind triggered by Intel, not a macro regime change — Intel's miss was idiosyncratic, and Nasdaq futures are already recovering 0.70% in pre-market. The real test is midterms Tuesday: a clean result either way removes an overhang. Positioning: selectively offensive in quality tech and BTC on any post-midterm dip, keep powder dry for the Friday jobs print.

The Binance hack is the wildcard. $6.3B is not systemic — Binance is operational — but the psychological damage to retail is real and the ETF outflows ($643M on Oct 30 alone) show institutional hedging. BTC holding above $77K through this is constructive. If midterms land without a contested result, I expect BTC to push toward $82–85K by mid-November. The 10Y yield at 4.93% is close to a critical level; a weak jobs number Friday could be the catalyst that breaks it lower and re-rates equities meaningfully.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial (Med Sci Sports Exerc) in well-trained male runners found that concurrent strength training significantly improves running economy durability — meaning you maintain efficiency under fatigue in the final stages of a race, not just at fresh paces. A 2026 narrative review in Quality in Sport confirmed strength training is a valuable injury-prevention component for distance runners, though it noted the injury data is still limited. Practical application: two weekly sessions of lower-body compound work (squat/deadlift variation + single-leg movement) on non-consecutive days, kept brief so it doesn't crowd out running mileage.
