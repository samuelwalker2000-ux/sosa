### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE announced in July 2026 that it is splitting Standard 90.1 — the benchmark commercial building energy efficiency standard — into two separate documents: one focused on energy use intensity (EUI) efficiency, and a new companion publication dedicated to operational carbon reduction. This decoupling matters because the two objectives often require conflicting mechanical strategies (e.g., a high-efficiency gas furnace reduces energy use but doesn't eliminate carbon). For MEP engineers, expect the companion carbon document to introduce requirements around electrification readiness, heat pump priority, and embodied carbon accounting in HVAC system selection — with the 2028 and 2031 cycles likely making these mandatory in jurisdictions that adopt ASHRAE 90.1 as their baseline energy code.
