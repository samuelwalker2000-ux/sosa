### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 62.1-2022 introduced the Indoor Air Quality Procedure (IAQP), which shifts the ventilation design framework from a prescriptive rate-based approach to a contaminant-specific performance approach. Under IAQP, designers model actual pollutant concentrations (PM2.5, VOCs, CO₂) rather than defaulting to a fixed CFM/person rate — this raises the bar for filtration and fan efficiency documentation but gives engineers flexibility to justify lower OA rates in well-filtered, lower-occupancy spaces. If you're designing office HVAC in jurisdictions adopting ASHRAE 62.1-2022 or later, get familiar with the IAQP pathway — it's increasingly relevant for energy-code compliance and tenant IAQ requirements.
