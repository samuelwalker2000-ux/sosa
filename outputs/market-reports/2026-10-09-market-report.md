### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy, low-rep strength training significantly improves running economy — even without body weight changes. A well-replicated study protocol had runners add 4 sets of 4-rep-max squats, three times weekly for 8 weeks, alongside their endurance training; the group showed measurable improvements in running economy versus endurance-only peers. Practical takeaway: sequence matters — lift before your run or on a separate day, as lifting after a hard run blunts strength adaptation (Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research).

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE is restructuring Standard 90.1 — its flagship energy efficiency standard for commercial buildings — by splitting energy efficiency requirements and carbon reduction rules into separate compliance frameworks. The stated goal is to simplify the compliance path for engineers and reduce confusion between energy use intensity targets and emissions-based metrics. This is a meaningful shift for MEP engineers: projects in jurisdictions that adopt the updated 90.1 may need to demonstrate compliance against both tracks independently rather than a single blended standard. Watch ASHRAE's news page (ashrae.org) for the official publication date and jurisdiction adoption timelines.
