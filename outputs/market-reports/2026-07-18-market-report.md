### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** July's heat is actually your performance secret weapon. Research consistently shows that just 10 days of deliberate heat training (running or exercising in warm conditions or a sauna post-workout) can improve VO2 max by 5–8% and time trial performance by 6–8% — and critically, those gains transfer to cool-weather races too. The mechanism is haematological: heat stress elevates haemoglobin mass and plasma volume, giving your cardiovascular system more oxygen-carrying capacity. If you're training through the summer, don't hide from the heat — lean into it deliberately and treat it as altitude training's cheaper sibling. (Source: Runners Connect; Journal of Physiology, Jenkins et al.)
