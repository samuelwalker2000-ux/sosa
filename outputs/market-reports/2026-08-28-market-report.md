### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial (*Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research*) confirmed that strength training improves **running economy durability** — not just fresh-leg efficiency, but the ability to maintain that efficiency deep into a run when fatigued. Specifically, eccentric-focused lower-body work (Romanian deadlifts, Nordic curls, slow-lowering split squats) remodels tendon tissue and reduces the energy cost of late-race strides. The practical takeaway: 2×/week of eccentric strength work integrated into your training block will pay off in the final kilometres of a race, not just in the gym.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The EPA's updated refrigerant transition rules — now in force for new HVAC equipment — tighten GWP (Global Warming Potential) limits for commercial split systems and rooftop units, accelerating the industry's shift away from HFC refrigerants like R-410A toward A2L alternatives (R-454B, R-32). The ASHRAE 2026 Annual Conference in Austin is addressing these directly, with sessions focused on updated ASHRAE 34 refrigerant safety classifications, A2L handling requirements (mildly flammable, requiring revised leak detection and ventilation protocols per ASHRAE 15), and updated OBC references for mechanical room design. If you're speccing HVAC systems now, confirm refrigerant type alignment with jurisdiction-specific code adoptions — Ontario and several US states are at different stages of the transition.
