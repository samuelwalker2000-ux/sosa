### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE published a structural split of Standard 90.1 in July 2026, separating energy efficiency compliance from carbon reduction frameworks into distinct pathways. This is a significant shift for MEP engineers: buildings can now demonstrate compliance via an energy-only path or a carbon-focused path, reducing the previous tension between electrification mandates and energy use intensity targets. In practice, this means mechanical designers on new projects should confirm with the AHJ which path is being followed early in schematic design — the equipment selection logic (heat pumps vs. gas-fired) flows from that choice.
