### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Two 30-minute strength sessions per week reduce running injury risk by 42% — but the key is running-specific loading (single-leg work, hip hinges, plyometrics), not generic gym training. For runners targeting speed, heavy load training (think: deadlifts, Bulgarian split squats, loaded step-ups) improves running economy most at higher paces (8.64–17.85 km/h), while plyometrics improve economy at easier paces. If you only have time for one type, prioritize heavy compound lower body work and you'll cover both speed and injury prevention bases.
