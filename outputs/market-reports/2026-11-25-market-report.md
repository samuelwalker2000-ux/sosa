The play is defensive — stay underweight tech, hold some GLD as a rates/risk hedge, and wait for the FOMC on December 9 before adding equity exposure.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research* study on masters trail runners found that combined uphill HIIT + strength training produced the broadest performance adaptation and was the protocol that most consistently improved 18km trail times. The practical takeaway: if you're a runner adding gym work, pairing heavy lower-body lifting with incline interval sessions delivers more than either modality alone — the neuromuscular adaptations from heavy loads (target ≥80% 1RM on squats/deadlifts) reduce ground contact time and energy cost at race pace. Two strength sessions per week is the minimum effective dose.
