### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy lower-body strength training (e.g., 4 sets × 4 reps of squats, 3x/week) improves running economy — the oxygen cost of running at a given pace — without requiring a change in body weight. The mechanism is improved neuromuscular efficiency and increased rate of force development, not hypertrophy. Scheduling lifting sessions on days separate from hard interval runs (or before easy runs rather than before speed work) maximises the adaptation without compromising quality running sessions.

*Sources: peer-reviewed research on concurrent training and running economy; confirmed in Doctors of Running's 2026 strength training podcast series.*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NEC) introduces a significant update requiring GFCI protection for outdoor HVAC equipment — previously this only applied to receptacles, not the units themselves. The NFPA granted a temporary exception to allow time for manufacturers to develop high-frequency (HF) GFCI devices compatible with variable-frequency drive (VFD) equipment; however, that exception expired September 1, 2026. Engineers designing new HVAC installations should confirm their jurisdiction's adopted NEC edition and verify the GFCI device availability for VFD-driven outdoor units before specifying, as non-compliant installations may fail inspection.

*Source: NFPA NEC 2026 update coverage via fixr.com; ASHRAE Standards Actions Aug 2026.*
