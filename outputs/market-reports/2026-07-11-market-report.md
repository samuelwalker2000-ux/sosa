Position: **patient**. Let the CPI print come in before adding risk. If July 14 CPI comes in at or above 4.2%, expect a sharp repricing in rates and tech. ### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial published via ResearchGate confirmed that strength training improves *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain better mechanics under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. The key takeaway for practical programming: eccentric-emphasis work (slow lowering phase, 3–4 seconds, with a pause at the stretched position) remodels tendon tissue and restores load tolerance, reducing injury risk while boosting late-race performance. Add 1–2 sessions per week of heavy eccentric squats, Romanian deadlifts, or Nordic curls to your run training block.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The EPA's HFC refrigerant phase-down under AIM Act is accelerating industry transition to lower-GWP alternatives — specifically R-32 (GWP ~675) and R-454B (GWP ~466) as direct A2L replacements for R-410A (GWP 2,088) in residential and commercial HVAC equipment. MEP engineers and contractors specifying new systems now must account for A2L flammability classification: updated ASHRAE 15 and updated equipment room ventilation requirements, ignition source mitigation, and refrigerant detector integration apply where R-454B or R-32 is used. If you're specifying chillers, VRF systems, or unitary equipment for projects breaking ground in 2026–2027, confirm the refrigerant type early — R-410A equipment production is being phased out and lead times for A2L-compatible equipment are extending.
