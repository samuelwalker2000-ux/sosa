### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric training — the slow, controlled lengthening of a muscle under load — is one of the most powerful tools for injury prevention in runners. It remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, improves landing mechanics, and reduces overuse injury risk. Practical application: add Nordic hamstring curls, slow single-leg calf raises (3s down), and Romanian deadlifts at a controlled 3–4 second eccentric tempo, 2–3x per week. Your joints will thank you come race season.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Effective September 1, 2026, new HVAC/R work in Texas and several other adopting jurisdictions must comply with the 2024 IMC (International Mechanical Code), 2024 IRC, IFGC, UMC, and ASHRAE 15-2024 / 34-2024. A particularly notable change: outdoor HVAC equipment is now required to have GFCI protection under four approved methods — this addresses years of nuisance-tripping complaints on equipment with variable-speed drives (VSDs), which generate leakage current that trips traditional GFCIs. MEP engineers should verify that new HVAC specs call for VSD-compatible GFCI solutions (e.g., equipment-grounding-conductor monitoring type) to avoid field rejection.
