### 2. MY TAKE

The midterm result is the clearest macro catalyst since the 2024 election. Democrats winning Congress flips the policy narrative: fiscal expansion stalls, infrastructure spending moderates, and defence procurement expectations reset lower. That explains the European defence selloff and the DXY weakness — the dollar is repricing a less hawkish fiscal outlook. The VIX collapsing 20% in a session tells you the market had priced in a contested result and didn't get one. This is a relief rally, not a new bull leg.

Be selective here. Crypto's jump is a short squeeze as much as genuine demand — ETF flows were net positive only because FBTC absorbed $237M while IBIT was actually bleeding. The Fear & Greed index sitting at 35 (Fear) despite a 5% BTC move tells you positioning is still defensive. I would not chase longs into resistance at $81K–$82K on BTC. For equities, the rotation into small caps and equal-weight names makes sense on reduced political risk, but the jobs report tomorrow is the real test — if payrolls disappoint, this relief rally gives back half. Stance: **patient** — let price consolidate and macro data confirm before adding risk.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners, DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0000000000003685) found that 10 weeks of supplementary strength training improved both running economy durability (maintaining efficiency deep into long runs) and high-intensity performance under fatigue compared to running-only training. The key practical implication: **heavy, low-rep work (3–5 sets of 3–5 reps at ≥80% 1RM)** appears to drive the neuromuscular adaptations that protect form and economy when tired, not high-rep circuits. Add 2 sessions/week in the base phase without cutting meaningful running volume.
