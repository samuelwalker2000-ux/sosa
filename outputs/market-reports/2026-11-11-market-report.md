### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE is overhauling Standard 90.1 by separating energy efficiency and embodied/operational carbon requirements into distinct sections — a structural change that allows code bodies (e.g., building codes, municipal authorities) to independently adopt the energy provisions, the carbon provisions, or both. This is significant for MEP designers in jurisdictions racing toward net-zero mandates: it means future project specifications may reference 90.1 energy compliance and a separate carbon pathway simultaneously, requiring MEP teams to coordinate HVAC system selection against both metrics rather than a single blended metric. Source: ACHR News (achrnews.com).
