### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 NEC (NFPA 70) includes a significant GFCI protection mandate effective **September 1, 2026**: all 208V and 240V single-phase HVAC equipment in single and multi-family residential buildings must now have GFCI protection installed. This catches many engineers off-guard because GFCI requirements historically applied to 120V circuits — the extension to HVAC-level voltages is a meaningful design change. Review your residential mechanical room drawings now if you have projects close to permit submission; adding GFCI protection to condensing unit circuits and split-system air handlers is no longer optional once jurisdictions adopt the 2026 NEC.
