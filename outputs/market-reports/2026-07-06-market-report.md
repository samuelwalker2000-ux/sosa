### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric loading — the slow, controlled lengthening of a muscle under load — is the single highest-ROI intervention for runners. A 2026 systematic review confirms it remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, and improves running economy across all training levels. Program 2 strength sessions per week (30-45 min) on easy run days, with 6+ hours separation from quality sessions. Priority moves: Romanian deadlifts, Bulgarian split squats, and eccentric-emphasis squats — these 3 cover ~80% of what a runner needs. Neglecting this is the most common path to overuse injury.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE has not yet published prescriptive specifications for liquid cooling in data centers — a fast-emerging gap as AI GPU compute density pushes beyond the limits of air cooling. Current ASHRAE TC 9.9 guidance covers air-side thermal envelopes but lacks defined coolant chemistry requirements, supply/return temperature ranges, or system pressure limits for rear-door heat exchangers and direct liquid cooling (DLC) loops. MEP engineers specifying AI data center cooling in 2026 are operating ahead of the formal standard and relying on manufacturer guidance. An ASHRAE Handbook supplement and formal TC 9.9 addendum addressing liquid cooling are expected by late 2026 or early 2027 — worth tracking if you're on any hyperscale or edge AI facility work.
