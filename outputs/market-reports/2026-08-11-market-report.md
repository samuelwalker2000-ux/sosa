### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomized controlled trial (*Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research*) confirms that supplementary strength training — maximal lifts plus plyometrics, twice weekly for 10 weeks — improves running economy not just at baseline but specifically after prolonged fatigue at marathon intensity. The key finding: the benefit is durability, not just efficiency. This means strength work pays off most in the back half of a race or long run, where untrained runners deteriorate. Two lifting sessions per week is all it takes — it doesn't cannibalize aerobic volume at that dose.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NEC) mandates that **all outdoor HVAC equipment must have GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) protection**, effective September 1, 2026. This applies to condensing units, heat pump compressors, and other outdoor mechanical equipment connected to electrical circuits. Engineers and contractors specifying or installing outdoor HVAC systems must ensure GFCI-compliant disconnect switches or breakers are included in designs — permits filed after the enforcement date without this protection will be flagged during inspection. Review project drawings now for compliance before the deadline.
