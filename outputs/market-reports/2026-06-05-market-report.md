### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2022 systematic review and meta-analysis (NIH/PMC9653533) confirmed that heavy resistance training improves running economy — the oxygen cost per unit distance at a given pace — which directly translates to faster race times without increasing VO2max. Specifically, 8–12 weeks of heavy lower-body lifts (squats, deadlifts, Romanian deadlifts) at ≥80% 1RM, 2–3 sessions per week, produced significant running economy gains in trained endurance runners. The practical takeaway: if you're running 4–5 days per week, adding 2 heavy strength sessions (not light/endurance lifting) is the highest-ROI intervention for race performance improvement.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2025 has raised the on-site renewable energy capacity requirement from 0.5 W/sq ft to 0.75 W/sq ft (Addendum aq) — a 50% increase — for commercial buildings complying with the prescriptive path. Alongside this, the standard introduced updated economizer provisions, lower minimum VAV airflow turndown ratios to reduce reheat energy waste, and expanded energy recovery requirements for HVAC systems. The energy monitoring section (Section 8) now mandates EMCS monitoring at 15-minute intervals with 36-month data retention across HVAC, lighting, plug loads, and process loads — critical for compliance documentation on any new commercial project.
