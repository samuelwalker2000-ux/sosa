### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE's 2026 conference and working groups have flagged a critical gap: the rapid adoption of liquid cooling for high-density AI server workloads is outpacing formal standards development. Engineers are deploying direct liquid cooling (DLC) and rear-door heat exchangers (RDHx) in data centres and large commercial buildings without clear ASHRAE 90.4 or ASHRAE 15 (Safety Code for Mechanical Refrigeration) guidance on secondary loop design, leak detection requirements, and glycol system integration. For MEP engineers in project delivery today, the practical advice is to reference ASHRAE TC 9.9 whitepapers and OEM documentation as interim guidance while formal standards catch up — and to specify double-wall heat exchangers to isolate IT fluid from chilled water loops as a risk mitigation measure.
