### 2. MY TAKE

Kevin Warsh's first FOMC press conference was the real story this week, and the market read it correctly as hawkish: stripping the 2026 rate cut from the dot plot while flagging hike risk for late 2026 is a regime change from the Powell-era reaction function, and the Nasdaq's -0.99% drop (versus the S&P's +0.33% bounce) tells you exactly where that repricing is landing — long-duration growth names, not value or industrials. Add a 12% VIX spike and you have a market that's nervous about the path, not the level, of rates. That's a genuine shift in the macro backdrop crypto and tech investors have been ignoring for months.

My stance: patient, leaning defensive on duration-sensitive tech specifically, not the broader market. The energy/industrials/consumer-defensive rotation (energy +22% YTD, industrials +16%) is the tell that smart money is already positioning for a "higher for longer, slower growth" world, and I'd rather rent that rotation than fight it by buying the Nasdaq dip into a hawkish Fed. On crypto, Extreme Fear at 23 with BTC still up on the week is the same setup as recent weeks — not a reason to panic-sell, but not a reason to add aggressively either until the post-Juneteenth volume returns and gives a cleaner read on whether $62–64K support actually holds.
