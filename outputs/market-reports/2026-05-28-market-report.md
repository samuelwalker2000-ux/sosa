### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ANSI/ASHRAE/IES Standard 90.1-2022 is now enforceable in multiple states (Alabama, Indiana, New Jersey, Oregon, Washington DC among early adopters) and introduces a mandatory energy monitoring requirement under Section 8: any commercial building exceeding 25,000 sq ft must install Energy Management Control Systems (EMCS) with metering that segregates HVAC, interior lighting, exterior lighting, plug loads, and process loads separately. Data must be collected at 15-minute intervals minimum and retained for 36 months — this is code, not guidance. For MEP engineers on new commercial projects, this means specifying dedicated submeter infrastructure early in schematic design or facing non-compliance during plan review; retrofit projects face the same requirement on permit-triggering renovations.
