### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 Ontario Building Code transition period is now complete, explicitly tying HVAC ventilation requirements to current ASHRAE 62.1 for the first time. A new provision (OBC 6.3.2.9) mandates outdoor air intakes be installed at least 0.3m above grade, and the code raises the bar on filtration, fan efficiency, and documented indoor air-quality verification. MEP teams with Ontario projects should re-check intake placement and ventilation calcs now, before permit submissions get kicked back for non-compliance.
