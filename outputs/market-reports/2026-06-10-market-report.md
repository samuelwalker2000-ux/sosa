### 2. MY TAKE

Crypto is getting hit far harder than equities right now, and that divergence is the story. BTC is down 17% on the week to $61.5K, ETH and SOL are both down mid-to-high teens over 7 days, and the Fear & Greed Index sitting at 10 (Extreme Fear) tells you sentiment has fully flipped from the euphoria of the prior all-time-high run near $122K. Meanwhile the S&P is basically flat on the day at 7,386. That's not "risk-off everywhere" — that's capital specifically leaving crypto for AI equities and de-risking ahead of CPI, while BTC ETF outflows (IBIT -$233M yesterday) confirm institutions are trimming too.

My positioning here is patient, not defensive and not aggressive. This is not the moment to chase weakness with leverage — Extreme Fear at single digits has historically marked local bottoms, but a hot CPI print today could easily push BTC toward $58-60K support before any real bounce. I'd rather watch the CPI reaction first, keep dry powder, and look for confirmation (BTC reclaiming $63K with volume, or ETF flows turning net positive) before adding. On equities, the S&P holding above 7,300 despite oil/dollar volatility is a sign of underlying resilience — that's the side of the market I'd lean toward if forced to deploy today.
