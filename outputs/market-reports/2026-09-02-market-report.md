### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 20-week combined endurance and strength training program showed a 4% improvement in running economy and a 4.6% increase in VO2max in a 2025 study (Applied Sciences). The key mechanism: strength training — especially velocity-based (power-focused) movements like explosive squats and plyometrics — improves neuromuscular efficiency, reducing the oxygen cost per stride. If you're only logging miles, you're leaving performance on the table. Add 2x/week of lower-body power work and expect meaningful gains over 12–16 weeks.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2025 (published March 2026) introduces a significant Energy Management Control System (EMCS) mandate: all covered buildings must now monitor electrical energy by load category — HVAC, interior lighting, exterior lighting, plug loads, and process loads — at 15-minute intervals minimum, with 36 months of data retention. Additionally, the standard raised on-site renewable capacity requirements from 0.5 to 0.75 W/sq ft (Addendum aq) and expanded healthcare facility unoccupied ventilation setbacks. For MEP designers, this means specifying submetering infrastructure and EMCS integration from design inception, not as an afterthought — especially on institutional and commercial projects targeting code compliance or LEED certification.
