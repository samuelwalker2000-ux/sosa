### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 narrative review in *Quality in Sport* confirms that supplementary strength training is a valuable injury-prevention strategy for distance runners — but the mechanism isn't fully understood. A 2025 randomised trial (28 well-trained men) found that a 10-week strength program allowed runners to sustain high-intensity running longer post-fatigue versus a running-only group. Practically: 2 lower-body strength sessions per week (squats, deadlifts, single-leg work + jumps) is the minimum effective dose — without cutting more than 10–15% of your running mileage to fit it in.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE is overhauling Standard 90.1 by separating energy efficiency requirements from operational-carbon reduction rules into two distinct frameworks. The move is significant for building engineers — 90.1 will continue as the energy-efficiency baseline (used in building codes globally), while a new parallel standard will address carbon intensity, decarbonisation pathways, and embodied carbon. For MEP teams, this means compliance paths will split: energy model submissions stay under 90.1, but carbon reporting on new builds (especially for LEED v5 / ESG-driven projects) will increasingly reference the new carbon standard. Begin tracking both.
