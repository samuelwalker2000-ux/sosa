### 2. MY TAKE

This is a stagflation tape, not a normal risk-off tape — and that distinction matters. Oil up on a real supply shock (Hormuz closure), CPI at a three-year high, gold falling instead of rallying, and bonds selling off alongside equities all point to the market repricing for "higher inflation + weaker growth" simultaneously, which is the worst regime for both stocks and long-duration risk assets. VIX spiking 40% in a day while the NASDAQ drops 4% tells you positioning was complacent into this and is now scrambling to de-risk.

My stance here is patient/defensive, not offensive. BTC holding near $61-62k support with RSI near 35 is interesting, but I wouldn't be adding size into a Fed meeting (June 16-17) that's the first under a new chair, with inflation data this hot — the Fed has every reason to stay on hold or even sound hawkish, which removes a key bull catalyst for risk assets. The one thing I'd watch closely is energy/oil-linked equities and inflation-protected positioning; everything else (growth tech, alts, gold) is fighting the macro current right now. Wait for either a de-escalation headline out of the Strait of Hormuz or a clear Fed signal before re-engaging offense.
