### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 is now the de facto national energy standard, with states required to certify adoption by early 2026 — and New York City's 2025 NYCECC (based on 90.1-2022) came into force on March 30, 2026. A critical new requirement for mechanical engineers: Section 8 mandates Energy Management Control Systems (EMCS) for any building exceeding 25,000 sq ft, with electrical energy monitoring broken down by load category (HVAC, lighting, plug loads, process) at minimum 15-minute intervals, retained for 36 months. This is a significant design shift — EMCS is no longer a value-add, it's a code compliance requirement. Any new commercial project in NYC or states that have adopted 90.1-2022 must incorporate sub-metering and BMS integration from concept design, not as a retrofit afterthought.
