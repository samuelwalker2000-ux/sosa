Crypto is the tell here, and it's not bullish. My stance: patient on crypto — this is accumulation territory for BTC if you have a multi-month horizon, but I wouldn't chase alts here. On equities, tactically offensive into the relief rally, but defensive sizing ahead of FOMC.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 is now the active national energy standard baseline, with states required to certify adoption by early 2026 — for buildings over 25,000 sq ft, Section 8 now mandates Energy Management Control Systems (EMCS) that log electrical energy by load category at 15-minute intervals with 36-month data retention. Paired with the ASHRAE 62.1-2022 Indoor Air Quality Procedure (IAQP), designers can now justify reduced outdoor air rates by demonstrating equivalent IAQ outcomes through closed-loop contaminant monitoring and verified air-cleaning tech — a meaningful lever for reducing HVAC energy load on retrofit projects while staying compliant.
