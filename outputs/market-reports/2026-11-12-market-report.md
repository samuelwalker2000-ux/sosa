### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy-load strength training (≥90% 1RM) and plyometrics are the most evidence-backed complement to distance running — they improve running economy, the efficiency at which you use oxygen at a given pace. A meta-analysis of middle- and long-distance runners found that heavy loads and explosive movements showed the most consistent performance gains, while moderate loads had weaker effects. Key takeaway: if you're adding gym work to your running, prioritise heavy compound lifts (squats, deadlifts) and plyometrics over machine circuits — and keep total running volume as the foundation.
