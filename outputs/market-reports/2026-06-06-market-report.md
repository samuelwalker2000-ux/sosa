### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomised controlled trial published in a peer-reviewed journal found that adding heavy strength training to a running program significantly improves *running economy durability* — specifically the ability to maintain efficient mechanics and pace during the final third of a race when fatigued, not just at fresh-legs paces. The key mechanism is eccentric loading: slow, controlled lengthening under load remodels tendon tissue, improves landing mechanics, and reduces injury risk. Two sessions per week of compound lower-body lifts (squats, Romanian deadlifts, single-leg work) is the evidence-backed dose.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2022 state certifications were due March 6, 2026, and the standard introduces mandatory energy monitoring for commercial buildings exceeding 25,000 sq ft. Under the new Section 8 requirement, an Energy Management Control System (EMCS) must monitor electrical energy *by load category* (HVAC, interior lighting, exterior lighting, plug loads, process loads) at minimum 15-minute intervals with 36 months of data retention. For MEP engineers, this means any new commercial building permit submitted after a state adopts 90.1-2022 must include EMCS metering specifications on the electrical drawings — a scope addition that's easy to miss during early design phases. The standard also sets ASHRAE on a path to carbon-neutral new construction by 2031, with each code cycle ratcheting efficiency targets.
