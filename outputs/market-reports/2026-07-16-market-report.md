The CPI print was the positive surprise of the week — inflation falling to 3.5% hands the Fed cover to shift tone at the July 29 meeting, and equities responded rationally by catching a bid. Don't get structurally bullish on risk yet — the macro is two-handed, and the geopolitical wildcard (Strait of Hormuz) is genuinely tail-risk territory.

The positioning stance here is **patient-to-defensive**. Cash or short-dated Treasuries while oil and the FOMC clarify. BTC holding $64k-plus with positive ETF inflows on July 15 ($163M net) is mildly constructive; if it breaks $65,100 with volume, that changes the calculus to offensive. Until then: watchlist, not conviction buys.
