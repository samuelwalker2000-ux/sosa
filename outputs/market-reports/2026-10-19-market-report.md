### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Runners consistently underestimate how long musculoskeletal adaptation takes relative to cardiovascular fitness gains. Aerobic capacity can improve meaningfully in 4–6 weeks, but tendons and connective tissue require months of gradual load — this mismatch is the primary driver of overuse injuries in recreational runners. The fix: cap weekly mileage increases at 10% and include one dedicated strength day targeting hip abductors, glutes, and calf eccentrics. (Source: The Conversation, Oct 8, 2026; supported by ACSM 2026 Fitness Trends)

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The Ontario 2024 Building Code (in force January 1, 2025, with April 2026 amendments) updated ventilation requirements to reference ASHRAE 62.1 directly, including new exhaust requirements tied to Section 6.5 of that standard, and added explicit garage ventilation provisions setting maximum contaminant concentration limits. For MEP engineers designing below-grade or enclosed parking, this means minimum exhaust rates are no longer left to "good engineering practice" — contaminant-based control (CO and NO₂ sensors) is now the expected design basis under the OBC. (Source: Ontario Building Code 2024 amendments, reviewed July 2026; Burban.ca OBC/HVAC summary)
