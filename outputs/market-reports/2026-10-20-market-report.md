### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized trial (*Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*) confirmed that a 10-week supplementary strength program significantly improves running economy and performance during prolonged effort in trained male runners. The key finding: benefits are most pronounced *after* fatigue sets in — so strength training doesn't just make you faster, it helps you hold pace in the back half of races. For practical application, 2 sessions/week of lower-body compound lifts (squats, Romanian deadlifts, hip thrusts) with loads of 4–6 reps is the evidence-backed template, keeping 1–2 reps in reserve.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2025 introduces new provisions for gas-fired heat pumps and gas-fired heat pump water heaters, allowing them to qualify for energy credits under the prescriptive path — a notable shift that acknowledges hybrid systems as a compliance route alongside all-electric solutions. The standard also updates equipment efficiency metrics from SEER/HSPF to SEER2/HSPF2 in all Section 6 tables, aligning with the Department of Energy's 2023 regulatory change. For MEP engineers specifying mechanical systems in 2026, this means energy models and equipment schedules need to be updated to SEER2/HSPF2 ratings — using the old metrics will produce non-compliant specifications in any jurisdiction that has adopted 90.1-2025.
