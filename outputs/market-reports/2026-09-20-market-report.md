### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Isometric and eccentric training is one of the most underutilised tools for runners — and 2025–26 research confirms it. Eccentric loading (slow, controlled 3–4 second lowering under tension, especially in a lengthened muscle position) remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, and measurably improves running economy when fatigued rather than just when fresh. Add slow Nordic curls, single-leg calf lowers, and Romanian deadlifts with a 4-second lowering phase to your weekly strength block — two sessions per week, separate from your hard run days. This is the difference between maintaining pace at km 15 and collapsing by km 12.
