### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eight weeks of supplementary strength training produces measurable running gains: a 5% improvement in running economy at 70% VO2max, a 21.3% increase in time to exhaustion at maximal aerobic speed, and reduced heart rate at lactate threshold — all without adding meaningful body weight. The key mechanism is improved neuromuscular efficiency and force production per stride. If you're a runner currently doing zero lifting, two sessions per week of compound lower-body work (squats, deadlifts, single-leg exercises) is the highest-ROI addition you can make to your training. *(Source: Loughborough University RCT, 2025)*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The A2L refrigerant transition took effect January 1, 2026 under the U.S. AIM Act and Kigali Amendment — most new HVAC equipment is now required to use A2L-classified refrigerants (e.g., R-32, R-454B, R-466A) in place of R-410A. A2L refrigerants have significantly lower global warming potential (GWP) but are classified as mildly flammable, requiring updated equipment design, installation practices, and service technician training per ASHRAE 15 and NFPA 1 provisions. If you're spec'ing or commissioning commercial HVAC systems in 2026, confirm that equipment, leak detection, and mechanical room ventilation comply with the new A2L safety requirements — legacy R-410A equipment may no longer be installed new.
