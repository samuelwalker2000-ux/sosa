### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial published in a peer-reviewed exercise physiology journal found that adding heavy strength training (3x/week, compound lifts) to an endurance running programme improved *running economy durability* — meaning athletes maintained their mechanical efficiency under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. The practical takeaway: eccentric loading (slow 3-4 second lowering phase on squats, RDLs, and calf raises) is the highest-ROI adaptation for runners — it remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, and cuts overuse injury risk. Two sessions per week is enough to see measurable benefit. *(Source: ResearchGate / Fisiologia del Ejercicio, 2025)*
