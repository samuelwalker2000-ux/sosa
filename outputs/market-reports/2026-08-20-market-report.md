### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy strength training can improve running economy durability — meaning you maintain better form and efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. A 2025 meta-analysis found that well-trained male runners who added two sessions per week of resistance training improved high-intensity performance in the fatigued state significantly more than their controls. The practical takeaway: don't just lift in the off-season — two sessions a week, year-round, with a focus on explosive power (not just maximum strength), translates directly to faster times and reduced injury risk.
