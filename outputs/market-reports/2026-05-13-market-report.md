Key finding: for maximal strength, train at ≥80% of your 1-rep max for 2–3 sets per exercise; for hypertrophy, prioritise total weekly volume (~10 sets per muscle group per week) over any single session intensity. ### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The R-410A refrigerant phase-out, which took effect in 2025, is now actively forcing HVAC equipment redesigns across the industry. OEMs have transitioned to A2L "mildly flammable" alternatives (e.g., R-32, R-454B), which require updated leak detection systems, revised electrical classification in mechanical rooms, and modified refrigerant-handling training for technicians. For MEP engineers on commercial projects, this means coordinating with mechanical contractors on code-compliant refrigerant management per ASHRAE 15 (Safety Standard for Refrigeration Systems) and ensuring electrical designs account for the revised hazardous area classifications around air-handling equipment.
