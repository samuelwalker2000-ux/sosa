Size up on the dip, keep stops tight into Tuesday/Wednesday.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 30-year study of more than 147,000 people, published in the British Journal of Sports Medicine, found that 90-120 minutes of resistance training per week is the "sweet spot" for reducing all-cause mortality risk, with the strongest benefits for cardiovascular and neurological disease. Combining that with aerobic work compounded the benefit further — so two to three solid 30-45 minute lifting sessions a week, paired with cardio, is enough; more isn't necessarily better.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Ontario's 2026 Building and Fire Code updates are tightening the explicit tie between mechanical ventilation design and ASHRAE 62.1 (Ventilation for Acceptable Indoor Air Quality), part of a broader harmonization with the National Building Code.
