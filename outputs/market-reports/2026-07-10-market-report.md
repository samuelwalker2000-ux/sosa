### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Post-exercise hot water immersion is a proven heat acclimation protocol with measurable performance benefits: 40 minutes of running at 65% VO2max followed by 40 minutes in a 40°C (104°F) bath, repeated over six consecutive days, significantly improves 5K time trial performance — not just in hot conditions, but in temperate ones too. The adaptation drives plasma volume expansion, lowered core temperature response, and improved sweat efficiency. If you're training through summer heat, lean into it strategically rather than avoiding it — it's free altitude training without the altitude. *(Source: Prolonged Heat Acclimation and Aerobic Performance, PMC6843002)*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 commercial HVAC energy codes — driven by IECC 2024 and updated ASHRAE 90.1 provisions — are now in effect and mandate an estimated 10–15% improvement in energy performance for new commercial buildings. The key levers are stricter equipment efficiency minimums (higher SEER2/EER2 ratings across all system types), expanded electrification requirements (heat pump priority in mixed-fuel climates), and tighter building envelope standards. For MEP engineers, this means equipment schedules and energy models on any new commercial permit submitted in 2026 must comply with the updated standards — and mechanical room layouts may need more space for dual-fuel or heat-pump hybrid equipment configurations that older designs didn't account for. *(Source: Bay Area SMACNA — 2026 HVAC Codes Now Effective; ManualJ Pro — 2026 Commercial HVAC Energy Codes Design Guide)*
