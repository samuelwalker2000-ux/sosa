### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NFPA 70) introduces a significant change to service sizing: load calculations have migrated to Article 130 with a new emphasis on Energy Management Systems (EMS) to prevent oversized electrical service installations — directly impacting how MEP engineers size panels and switchgear in commercial projects. Critically, arc-flash safety labelling is now **mandatory for all service equipment regardless of amperage** (previously only required above certain thresholds), meaning HVAC technicians and electrical engineers must ensure all service panels are properly labelled during design and commissioning. For Canadian projects under the Ontario Building Code, align with CSA C22.1-2024 (the Canadian Electrical Code) which incorporates similar arc-flash requirements — worth cross-checking scope applicability on mixed-jurisdiction projects.
