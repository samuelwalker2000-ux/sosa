### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A September 2026 trial found that adding strength and plyometric work twice a week for 10 weeks improved running economy specifically in the late stages of a 90-minute run — the benefit didn't appear when runners were fresh. The implication for distance runners: strength training pays off most when you're fatigued, so it's most valuable when training for marathon-length or longer efforts. Keep strength sessions away from your hardest running days and prioritise hip stability and posterior-chain work (deadlifts, split squats, single-leg RDLs) over upper body volume.
