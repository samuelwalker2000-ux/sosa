### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** **ASHRAE 90.1-2022 State Adoption Deadline — Early 2026.** States were required to certify adoption of ASHRAE 90.1-2022 by early 2026, making this the governing energy standard for most new commercial building projects. Key HVAC implications: the standard introduces **SEER2/HSPF2 metrics** for air-cooled heat pumps under 65,000 BTU/hr capacity (replacing legacy SEER/HSPF), mandates improved Total System Performance Ratios (TSPR), and requires dedicated outdoor air systems (DOAS) in more applications. A 2025 addendum also increased on-site renewable energy capacity requirements from 0.5 to 0.75 W/sq ft — important for rooftop solar integration in mechanical equipment rooms. If you're specifying HVAC equipment or reviewing energy models, confirm you're using 90.1-2022 efficiency thresholds and the correct metric (SEER2 not SEER) or risk non-compliance.
