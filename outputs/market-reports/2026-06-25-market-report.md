### 2. MY TAKE

The split screen here is the story: equities are rallying into the open on a hardware-demand beat from Micron, while crypto sentiment sits at Extreme Fear (17) and BTC is down nearly 3% on the week. That's not noise — it's the AI trade rotating within itself (chips up on fundamentals, speculative risk capital pulling out of crypto) at the exact moment a new, more hawkish Fed chair is openly discussing rate hikes instead of cuts. Warsh's task forces and refusal to give forward guidance add a layer of policy uncertainty that crypto, as the most liquidity-sensitive asset class, is pricing first.

My stance here is patient, leaning defensive on crypto specifically. I wouldn't chase the equity premarket pop blind — it's one earnings beat, not a trend reversal, and today's PCE print can erase it by 9am. On BTC, Extreme Fear plus a hawkish Fed is historically a "wait for confirmation, not a knife-catch" setup — I'd rather see BTC reclaim and hold above $98K with dominance stable before adding, than buy the fear reading outright. Cash and dry powder are the right position into a PCE print and a Fed that just told you it might not be done hiking.
