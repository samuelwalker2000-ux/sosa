### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 62.1-2022 introduced the **Indoor Air Quality Procedure (IAQP)** as a credible alternative to the traditional Ventilation Rate Procedure (VRP). IAQP allows engineers to design HVAC systems that achieve equivalent or superior indoor air quality outcomes with *less outdoor air*, by using third-party-verified air-cleaning technologies and closed-loop IAQ monitoring. As states and municipalities increasingly adopt codes aligned with ASHRAE 62.1-2022, MEP engineers who can demonstrate IAQP compliance unlock significant energy savings — particularly relevant for dense commercial spaces and retrofits where outdoor air intake is constrained. Worth knowing for any project where ventilation quantities are being scrutinized by AHJ or targeting LEED/WELL credits.
