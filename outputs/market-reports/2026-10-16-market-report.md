### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised trial (Zanini et al.) confirmed that a 10-week supplementary strength training programme significantly improves running economy (RE) in both nonfatigued and fatigued states at marathon intensity — meaning you'll hold better form late in a race. The sweet spot is 2 sessions/week of single-leg, eccentric, and hip-stability work layered onto your existing run volume; heavy bilateral squats alone show weaker transfer to RE than unilateral and sport-specific patterns. Bottom line: add single-leg RDLs, Bulgarian split squats, and eccentric calf raises to your plan and run faster at the same perceived effort within 8–10 weeks.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE released an advisory addendum to Standard 90.1-2025 (addendum "o") targeting equipment sequencing logic for multiple heating units serving the same load — the public review closed September 6, 2026. A separate addendum to Guideline 36-2024 fixes a demand-controlled ventilation (DCV) logic bug specifically in dual duct boxes using "cold deck minimum" logic, which in some configurations was allowing excessive outside air at part-load conditions. If your projects include dual duct VAV systems with GL36 controls, review this addendum; incorrectly implemented DCV in dual duct systems is a common energy compliance gap that also affects LEED and ASHRAE 189.1 calculations.
