### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** As of January 1, 2026, the US EPA AIM Act HFC phasedown effectively banned R-410A in newly manufactured residential and light commercial HVAC equipment — all new systems must now use refrigerants below 700 GWP. The primary replacements are R-454B (GWP ~466) and R-32 (GWP 675), both classified as A2L (mildly flammable) under ASHRAE Standard 34. This classification matters for designers: A2L equipment requires specific ventilation provisions, detection systems in enclosed spaces, and installer qualification — check your jurisdiction's adoption of ASHRAE 15-2022 and local amendments before specifying new equipment, particularly for rooftop units and split systems in mechanical rooms.
