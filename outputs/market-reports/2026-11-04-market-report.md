### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 183-2023 (Peak Cooling and Heating Load Calculations in Buildings) has now been formally adopted into energy codes across most U.S. jurisdictions as of late 2026, replacing older load calculation references. For MEP engineers, this means design cooling loads must follow the ASHRAE 183 methodology — integrated with ASHRAE 90.1 and local energy codes — which shifts some calculations around internal heat gains, envelope performance, and coincident peak timing. If you're using legacy load calculation software (older Carrier HAP or Trane TRACE versions), verify that the latest updates align with Standard 183 to avoid compliance issues on new permit submissions.
