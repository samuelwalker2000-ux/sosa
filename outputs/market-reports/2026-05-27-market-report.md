### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial found that strength training improves not just baseline running economy but *running economy durability* — athletes who lift maintain their movement efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. Two well-programmed sessions per week (squat, Bulgarian split squat, Romanian deadlift, plank) cover ~80% of what distance runners need. If you're only running and wondering why you fade in the final miles, the answer is almost certainly missing eccentric strength work — slow, controlled lowering under load remodels tendon tissue and restores load tolerance in a way aerobic training alone cannot.
