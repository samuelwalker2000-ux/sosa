### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2025/2026 Ontario Building Code (OBC) now mandates a **Mechanical Ventilation Design Summary (MVDS)** for every new residential build — a formal document proving a code-compliant heat recovery ventilation (HRV/ERV) system has been designed and installed. Critically for cold-climate projects, heat pumps must now demonstrate rated capacity at the local design temperature using CSA F280 load calculations (the same standard as a furnace), closing a previous gap that allowed undersized heat pump installs. This aligns OBC with the broader trend: heat pumps outsold fossil fuel furnaces in Canada for the fourth consecutive year in 2025, and new code is now forcing the design rigour to match adoption pace.
