### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial (*Applied Sciences*) confirmed that strength training twice per week improves not just baseline running economy but *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain efficiency at faster paces under fatigue, translating to better late-race performance. The mechanism is increased propulsive force from improved neuromuscular recruitment, not just hypertrophy. Prioritise compound lower-body work (squats, deadlifts, single-leg RDLs) at moderate-to-heavy loads (60–80% 1RM) in two sessions/week during your training block.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The US EPA's 2026 refrigerant transition rules are now in effect, reshaping HVAC specifications across commercial and residential projects. The rules mandate phasedown of high-GWP HFCs (R-410A and similar) and require installers to qualify for low-GWP alternatives (A2L refrigerants like R-32 and R-454B). For MEP engineers and contractors, this has practical implications: new refrigerant-specific flammability requirements, revised leak detection protocols, and updated training/certification obligations. The 2026 ASHRAE Annual Conference in Austin highlighted that ground source heat pumps using low-GWP refrigerants are emerging as the preferred decarbonization pathway for commercial buildings — increasingly written into new project specifications and local energy codes.
