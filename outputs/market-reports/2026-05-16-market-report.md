### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial published in a peer-reviewed journal found that strength training improves **running economy durability** — meaning trained runners maintain their mechanical efficiency deep into a race when fatigued, not just in fresh conditions. The practical takeaway: two strength sessions per week for 10–14 weeks (targeting knees, hips, and foot/ankle complexes) produces measurable gains in late-race performance. Combined endurance + strength programming consistently outperforms either modality alone, suggesting these adaptations are synergistic, not competing. If you're only running to get better at running, you're leaving performance on the table.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE is actively developing **Standard 240P** — *Evaluating Greenhouse Gas (GHG) and Carbon Emissions in Building Design, Construction and Operation* — which will formalize whole life cycle (WLC) carbon assessment methodology for building systems. This matters practically: jurisdictions are increasingly requiring carbon accounting at the permit stage, and ASHRAE 240P will likely become the referenced standard for compliance. MEP engineers should expect WLC carbon analysis to become a deliverable alongside energy models within the next 1–2 code cycles, particularly for institutional and commercial projects. Start familiarizing with the methodology now — the tools and workflows are immature, and early movers will have a consulting edge.
