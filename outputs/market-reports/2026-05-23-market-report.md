### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Combining heavy resistance training with plyometric work is the most evidence-backed method for improving running economy — a 2022 meta-analysis in *Sports Medicine* confirmed both modalities independently reduce the oxygen cost of running at a given pace. The mechanism is improved neuromuscular recruitment and tendon stiffness, not simply getting stronger. Practical application: add 2x/week of either heavy squats (3–5 sets of 3–5 reps) or box jumps/bounding to your training block, separated from your hard running days.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 — now the national reference standard with states required to certify adoption by early 2026 — mandates Energy Management Control Systems (EMCS) for all commercial buildings exceeding 25,000 sq ft (Section 8). The requirement includes measurement devices monitoring electrical energy use by load category (HVAC, lighting, plug loads) at a minimum 15-minute interval, with data retained for 36 months. For MEP engineers: any new design or major renovation above the threshold must now include a compliant EMCS in the design deliverables or the project fails code review. Budget line items and specification sections need to account for this — it's no longer optional.
