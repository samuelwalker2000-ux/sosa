### 2. MY TAKE

This isn't a crypto-specific drawdown — it's a synchronized risk-off across every speculative corner of the market at once. BTC at a 20-month low with six consecutive days of ETF outflows is the headline, but gold breaking below $4,000 and Mag7 names like Apple and Microsoft getting hit on AI-capex cost fears tells you capital is pulling in from the edges generally, not rotating into a safe haven. Layer on a hot PCE print (4.1%, three-year high) landing right as the Fed signals hikes instead of cuts, and you get the textbook setup for de-leveraging: no rate-cut catalyst, no yield-curve relief, and the marginal buyer (ETF flows) actively walking away.

My stance is defensive and patient, not bottom-fishing. The AI trade itself is genuinely mixed — Bloomberg's data show AI sales are finally starting to outpace data-center depreciation for the first time in two quarters, which is a real fundamental signal — but the market is currently pricing the capex bill, not the revenue catch-up, and that repricing has further to run while ETF outflows are still accelerating. I'd want to see the BTC ETF outflow streak break and price reclaim and hold $62K–65K before treating this as a bottom rather than a falling knife. Cash and dry powder remain the right position into next week's NFP print (July 2) and a Fed that just told you it isn't done hiking.
