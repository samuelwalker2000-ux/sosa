A break below $82K on heavy volume means another leg down toward $75K before year-end.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy strength training (≥85% 1RM squats, deadlifts, RDLs) twice weekly significantly improves running economy — multiple studies show 4-8% improvement in oxygen cost at sub-maximal paces without meaningful increases in body mass. The mechanism is neuromuscular: heavier loads train the nervous system to recruit more muscle fibers per stride, reducing the effort per footfall. If you're currently only doing high-rep bodyweight or light-load gym work alongside running, you're leaving performance on the table — swap two sessions per cycle to true strength work.
