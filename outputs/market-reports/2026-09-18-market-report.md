### 2. MY TAKE

The Fed finally moved — 25 bps, unanimous, first hike in three years. Markets are treating this like a relief valve: equities are green, VIX is sliding, and the dollar is softer. That's the textbook post-priced-in-hike playbook, and right now it's playing out cleanly. But the dot plot is hawkish — 16 of 18 participants see at least one more hike, and year-end projections cluster between 4.1%–4.4%. The 10Y at 4.95% is doing the heavy lifting. Oil at $100+ keeps inflation sticky. The "one-and-done" narrative is premature.

Positioning: lean **patient-to-offensive** in risk assets short-term on post-FOMC momentum, but don't chase. BTC at $76K is consolidating — it's not broken but not building either. The Sep 26 PCE is the next trigger. If PCE comes in hot, rate expectations re-price fast and this rally reverses. Keep dry powder. Equities over crypto right now — equity risk premium is clearer, and the Fear & Greed at 31 signals crypto still hasn't shaken post-summer weakness.

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (ResearchGate, Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability) confirmed that strength training doesn't just improve your fresh-state running economy — it specifically preserves efficiency under fatigue. Runners who trained with moderate-to-heavy loads (50–80% 1RM, squats and deadlifts) maintained better propulsive force and ground contact time in the final miles of long runs compared to endurance-only controls. The practical takeaway: two strength sessions per week focused on compound lower-body movements is the minimum effective dose for runners who want to stop slowing down late in a race.
