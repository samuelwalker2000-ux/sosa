### 2. MY TAKE

Bitcoin's failure to participate in last week's equity melt-up is the tell here, not noise. The S&P ripped 1%+ and the Nasdaq nearly 2% on Iran de-escalation and chip-stock relief, while BTC sat 2% below where it started the week and Fear & Greed sank to 25. That divergence says institutional risk appetite is flowing into names people already understand (mega-cap tech, financials, industrials) rather than into crypto, which still trades like a leveraged-beta asset that gets sold first when nothing scary is actually happening. ETF flows back this up — Friday's complex bled $82M with ARKB leading outflows, and there's been no clean reversal since.

My stance here is patient, not defensive. BTC is sitting on the exact support shelf ($60K–62K) that has held for weeks, and the macro backdrop — Iran talks progressing, oil easing, a Fed that just held rates without panic — is constructive, not hostile. I wouldn't chase alts into this tape; the "rally" in names like AERO and JUP is short-lived narrative rotation, not broad risk-on. The asymmetric trade is waiting for a reclaim of $64K–65K on BTC with volume before adding, while letting equities' industrial/energy/consumer-defensive rotation run since that trade has actual earnings and Fed-policy logic behind it.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022's Appendix L introduces the Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR) pathway — a shift from prescriptive, component-by-component HVAC compliance to evaluating the efficiency of the entire system as an integrated whole. For designers, this opens flexibility to use higher-efficiency heat pumps or heat-recovery chillers that wouldn't individually meet prescriptive minimums, as long as the whole-system ratio clears the bar. NYC enforcement of 2025 NYCECC / ASHRAE 90.1 begins March 30, 2026, so TSPR modeling is worth building into project workflows now.
