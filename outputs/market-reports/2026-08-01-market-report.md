### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 20-week strength training program for well-trained male runners produced meaningful performance gains: running economy improved 4%, relative VO₂max increased 4.6%, and maximal aerobic speed rose 2.8% (ResearchGate, 2025 RCT). The key mechanism is running economy *durability* — strength-trained runners maintain efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. Focus on moderate-to-heavy loads (50–80% 1RM) with particular emphasis on eccentric work (slow lowering phase) to remodel tendons and cut injury risk. Two sessions per week alongside your running programme is sufficient.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1 (2025 edition) — now widely adopted — has codified unoccupied setbacks for certain clinical spaces in health care facilities, a provision previously left to engineering judgement. MEP engineers on healthcare projects need to account for these setbacks in BMS/DDC programming and ventilation schedules; failure to do so risks code non-compliance at final inspection. Simultaneously, the 2026 NEC (National Electrical Code) has shifted load calculation methodology toward Article 130, which emphasises Energy Management Systems (EMS) integration — making coordinated MEP BMS design not just best practice but a code requirement on new builds. The practical impact: electrical and mechanical scopes now share a common EMS backbone, and those two disciplines need to be coordinated from schematic design rather than at the DD/CD clash-resolution stage.
