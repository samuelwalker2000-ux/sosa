### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2025 was published in March 2026, incorporating 105 addenda that represent the most extensive update to the commercial building energy standard in years. A key change: formal recognition and prescriptive requirements for **gas-fired heat pumps** and **gas-fired heat pump water heaters**, expanding the heat pump compliance pathway beyond electric-only systems. The standard also updates baseline HVAC system definitions with new distinctions based on heating source, and tightens dehumidification and ventilation requirements in alignment with ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 62.1. If you're designing commercial HVAC systems in 2026, this edition is the new reference — check which jurisdictions are adopting it vs. remaining on 90.1-2022.
