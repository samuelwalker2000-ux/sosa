### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy and plyometric strength training improves running economy by roughly 2–8% in distance runners, while light high-rep work shows little to no measurable benefit (meta-analysis, Quality in Sport, July 2026). The mechanism is neuromuscular: heavier loads increase motor unit recruitment and tendon stiffness, making each stride more efficient. For runners, 2–3 sessions of heavy lower-body work (squats, deadlifts, single-leg press) plus plyometrics per week is the evidence-based sweet spot — not cardio-adjacent circuits.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE has opened the first public review of BSR/ASHRAE 244P / CSA R400:2026 — a new joint ASHRAE/CSA standard establishing *product category rules* for HVAC&R-related MEP equipment, covering lifecycle assessment criteria. The comment period runs through December 1, 2026. This is worth tracking: product category rules (PCRs) underpin Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), and as embodied carbon requirements increasingly appear in building codes and green certification programs (LEED, WELL, Toronto Green Standard), having a consistent HVAC&R PCR framework will affect how engineers specify and compare mechanical systems on regulated projects.
