### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Recent research (2025-2026) consistently shows that strength training improves *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain their oxygen efficiency under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. A randomized controlled trial found that a combined endurance + strength programme produced a 4–4.6% improvement in running economy at matched aerobic output. The practical takeaway: moderate-to-heavy load lifting (50–80% 1RM, 2x/week) is the minimum effective dose for runners — it's not competing with mileage, it's protecting it.
