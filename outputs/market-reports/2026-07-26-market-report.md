### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (*Fisiologia del Ejercicio*) confirmed that concurrent strength training significantly improves running economy, durability, and high-intensity performance in well-trained male runners. The mechanism is neuromuscular: heavier, lower-rep compound lifts (squats, deadlifts, hip thrusts) recruit more motor units and reduce the energy cost of each stride. If you're a runner skipping the gym, you're leaving free speed on the table — 2×/week of strength work is the minimum effective dose.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Under the 2026 NEC (National Electrical Code), Article 130 now governs load calculations with a shift toward Energy Management Systems (EMS) — load calculations must account for EMS-controlled demand reduction rather than simply summing connected loads. This means MEP engineers specifying electrical service for commercial buildings can design for smaller upstream gear (transformers, switchgear) if an EMS is part of the system, reducing upfront capital cost. Additionally, arc-flash hazard labelling is now mandatory on all service equipment regardless of amperage — a compliance point to flag on any existing facility assessment or new construction specification.
