### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial (*ResearchGate / Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research*) found that a 20-week combined endurance + strength training program improved running economy by 4%, VO2max by 4.6%, and speed at ventilatory thresholds by 3.7–9.4% in well-trained male runners — specifically noting that the gains held under fatigue (late-race economy), not just in fresh conditions. The practical takeaway: if you only run, you're leaving your last-mile performance on the table. Add two strength sessions per week (compound lifts + eccentric-focused lower body work) during your base phase, not just race prep.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 National Electrical Code (NEC) introduces a new requirement that all outdoor HVAC equipment — including condensing units, heat pumps, and packaged rooftop units — must have GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) protection, with full enforcement taking effect September 1, 2026. This affects spec writers, contractors, and engineers alike: existing disconnects without GFCI must be upgraded, and high-frequency (HF) GFCIs compatible with variable-frequency-drive (VFD) equipment are required to avoid nuisance tripping. If you're stamping drawings for projects with permit submissions after Sept 1 in jurisdictions that have adopted 2026 NEC, GFCI on all outdoor HVAC disconnects is non-negotiable.
