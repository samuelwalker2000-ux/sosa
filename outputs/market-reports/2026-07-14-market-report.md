### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1 (2025 edition) now includes **Addendum bh**, which mandates unoccupied setbacks in certain clinical spaces within healthcare facilities — a change with significant mechanical system implications. Systems serving clinical areas must now be designed to modulate ventilation and thermal delivery during unoccupied periods, which historically operated at full design airflow 24/7 for infection control reasons. For MEP engineers, this means demand-controlled ventilation (DCV) strategies and VAV systems with occupancy sensing need to be incorporated into healthcare designs to comply with the standard, while still satisfying ASHRAE 170 (Ventilation of Health Care Facilities) minimum requirements. The practical implication: early coordination between mechanical and BAS/controls engineers is now critical on any hospital or healthcare fit-out project to verify that setback sequences comply with both 90.1-2025 and 170 simultaneously.
