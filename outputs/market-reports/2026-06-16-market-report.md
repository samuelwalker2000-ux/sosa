### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric training — the slow, controlled lengthening of a muscle under load — is one of the most powerful and underused tools for both injury prevention and performance in runners. Research consistently shows it strengthens tendons (not just muscle fibers), directly addressing the Achilles, patellar, and IT band issues that sideline distance runners most often. A simple protocol: 3 sets of 8–10 slow (3–4 second) lowering-phase single-leg calf raises or Nordic hamstring curls, 2–3x per week, is enough to see measurable tendon stiffness improvements in 6–8 weeks. *(Source: Track & Field News / PubMed meta-analysis on strength training for distance runners)*
