### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 RCT in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* found that a 10-week supplementary strength program in well-trained male runners improved both running economy durability and fatigued high-intensity performance — meaning the gains show up late in races when it counts most. The practical takeaway: two short strength sessions per week (30-40 mins, compound lower-body focus — squats, deadlifts, single-leg work) are enough to get the adaptation without accumulating excessive fatigue. Don't skip it in training blocks where you're already running high volume; the interference effect is minor compared to the late-race benefit.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 edition of NFPA 10 (Standard for Portable Fire Extinguishers) now permits electronic monitoring systems to supplement or replace some manual inspection checks previously required by building personnel. For MEP engineers on commercial and institutional projects, this opens the door to integrated BAS-connected monitoring for extinguisher status — reducing manual compliance overhead and supporting continuous commissioning workflows. Key consideration: the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) must approve the technology, and facility documentation requirements remain unchanged. Worth flagging on any new-build spec where NFPA 10 compliance is a deliverable.
