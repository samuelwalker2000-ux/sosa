### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomized controlled trial published in the Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research confirms that 8–12 weeks of heavy lower-body resistance training (hip extensors, knee extensors, ankle plantar flexors) improves running economy by improving neuromuscular efficiency — you run faster at the same oxygen cost. The underrated mechanism: strength training increases tendon stiffness, which improves elastic energy return on every ground contact. Practical application: add 2× weekly heavy compound lifts (trap bar deadlift, Bulgarian split squat, calf raises with load) and your 5K/10K pace will improve without adding more running volume.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 commercial HVAC codes (IECC 2024 + forthcoming ASHRAE 90.1 update) mandate a full transition to A2L refrigerants (R-32, R-454B) for virtually all new commercial HVAC installations. A2L refrigerants are classified as "mildly flammable" — meaning design teams must now comply with stricter safety standards for equipment rooms, refrigerant detection systems, and ventilation requirements under ASHRAE 15. For MEP engineers, this changes the equipment selection process: specify A2L-rated equipment from the start, confirm ventilation design for refrigerant concentration limits, and ensure AHJ approval pathways are mapped early given the still-evolving local adoption of these codes. Oversizing any A2L system is now both a compliance and safety liability.
