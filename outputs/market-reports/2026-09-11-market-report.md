### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 NEC (NFPA 70) now requires **HF-rated GFCI protection** on circuits serving high-efficiency HVAC equipment that uses inverter-based variable-speed drives. Standard GFCI devices can experience nuisance tripping with the high-frequency leakage currents generated by modern VFDs and heat pump compressors — HF-rated devices are designed to filter these frequencies without sacrificing personnel protection. This is a practical compliance point for any commercial or residential HVAC upgrade project in 2026: specify HF-rated GFCI at the panel for inverter-driven equipment to avoid callback calls and failed inspections.
