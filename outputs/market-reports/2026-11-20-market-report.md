### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* study found that adding heavy lower-body strength training and plyometrics to a normal running programme improved running economy — but specifically in the *later stages* of a long run (after ~60–70 minutes), not when athletes were fresh. The implication: strength work doesn't make you more efficient off the gun; it makes you hold form and economy when fatigued. Practical takeaway: two 45-minute lower-body strength sessions per week (squats, deadlifts, single-leg work, box jumps) added on top of your existing mileage — not in place of it — to target late-run efficiency.
