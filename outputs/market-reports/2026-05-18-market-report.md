### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2025 has introduced a critical update requiring Energy Recovery Ventilation (ERV) systems to achieve ≥80% sensible heat recovery efficiency across a wider range of building types — a significant jump from previous thresholds. States are required to certify adoption by early 2026, meaning projects in design now will need to specify and size ERV units to this new benchmark or justify alternatives under the performance path. For MEP engineers, this means re-evaluating standard ERV product selections: many existing packaged units fall in the 70–75% efficiency range and will no longer satisfy prescriptive compliance. Cross-flow plate or counter-flow energy wheel configurations are the go-to solutions for hitting ≥80%.

*Source: ASHRAE 90.1-2025 / Refria ERV Compliance Guide*
