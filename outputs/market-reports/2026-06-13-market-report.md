### 2. MY TAKE

Equities are partying like it's 2021 on the back of one mega-IPO while crypto is pricing in a recession. That gap doesn't close quietly — either equities catch down to the risk-off signal crypto is sending, or crypto is wildly oversold and due a violent reflexive bounce once the FOMC week passes without a hawkish shock. Given Fed funds futures are now flirting with a *hike* narrative again, I'd bet on the former: this SpaceX-driven tech rotation has the smell of a late-cycle blow-off, not a fresh leg up.

My stance here is patient-to-defensive. I'm not chasing the SpaceX pop or piling into beaten-down alts on a "Fear and Greed at 12 means buy" reflex — extreme fear can persist for weeks in a genuine deleveraging, and ETH's dominance grinding toward multi-year lows (8.94%) tells you capital is still leaving risk curve, not rotating within it. Hold core BTC, keep dry powder for a post-FOMC re-entry, and treat this week's CPI/PPI-to-FOMC sequence as the actual catalyst — not the SpaceX headline noise.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A University of Copenhagen study found that "10-20-30" interval training — 30 seconds easy, 20 seconds moderate, 10 seconds near-maximal effort, repeated — produces running performance and fitness gains comparable to traditional interval sessions, even when the hard efforts are dialed back to ~80% intensity. Worth swapping in once or twice a week if you're managing fatigue but don't want to lose speed work.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** Ontario's 2026 Building and Fire Code updates (in effect since January 1, 2026) now explicitly tie ventilation compliance to ASHRAE 62.1 for Acceptable Indoor Air Quality — a notable hardening of the IAQ requirements for commercial HVAC design in the province. Combined with the broader ASHRAE 90.1-2022 rollout (which mandates Energy Management Control Systems with 15-minute interval monitoring and 36-month data retention for buildings over 25,000 sq ft), projects now need to budget for both ventilation rate verification against 62.1 and EMCS metering infrastructure at the design stage — this is no longer a "value engineer it out later" item.
