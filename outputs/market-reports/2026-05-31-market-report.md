### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomized controlled trial confirms that adding twice-weekly heavy strength training to a running programme improves *running economy durability* — i.e., runners maintain their metabolic efficiency under fatigue in the final miles rather than just when fresh. The mechanism is improved neuromuscular coordination and type I/II muscle fiber strength, which reduces energy cost per stride at race pace. Prioritise compound lower-body lifts (squats, Romanian deadlifts, leg press) at 80–85% 1RM for 3–4 sets of 4–6 reps, twice per week during base training blocks.

*Source: ResearchGate — "Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners" (RCT, 2025/2026)*
