### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Strength training improves *running economy durability* — not just fresh-leg efficiency, but how well your economy holds up over a long run. A 2025 RCT found that 10 weeks of twice-weekly strength and plyometric work reduced running economy degradation over 90 minutes from -4.7% to -2.1% in the strength-trained group. The practical takeaway: 2–3 sessions per week of heavy lower-body compound lifts (squats, deadlifts) plus jumps is the minimum effective dose to maintain form and efficiency in the back half of a long run or race. Source: *"Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners"* (RCT, 2025).
