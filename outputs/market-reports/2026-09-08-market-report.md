### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Eccentric training — the slow, controlled lowering phase of a movement under load — is one of the most effective tools for both injury prevention and performance gains in runners. Research confirms it remodels tendon tissue, restores load tolerance, and improves landing mechanics. Practically: add slow Nordic hamstring curls and single-leg heel drops (3 sets of 8 reps, 3–5 second lowering phase) to your weekly routine; 2x/week is sufficient to see measurable tendon adaptation within 6 weeks. Source: ScienceDaily / Sports Medicine research, 2026.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE Standard 90.1-2025 (published March 2026, ANSI approved) introduces mandatory Energy Management Control System (EMCS) requirements under Section 8: buildings must now monitor electrical energy by load category — HVAC, interior lighting, exterior lighting, plug loads, and process loads — at minimum 15-minute intervals, with 36-month data retention. This is a significant compliance shift for commercial projects; mechanical engineers need to coordinate with electrical and controls teams early in design to ensure metering infrastructure is specified correctly, as many legacy building automation systems won't meet the granularity requirement without upgrades.
