### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** The ACSM published its first major update to resistance training guidelines in 17 years (April 2026, synthesising 137 systematic reviews and 30,000+ participants). Key finding: for **hypertrophy**, you need at least **10 sets per muscle group per week** — and loads from 30–100% of 1RM all work, provided each set is taken close to failure (roughly 2–3 reps in reserve; going to absolute failure adds fatigue without extra benefit). For **strength**, prioritise ≥80% 1RM, full range of motion, 2–3 sets, done first in the session. The big headline: *any* consistent resistance training — band-based, home-based, circuit-style — produces meaningful gains. Stop optimising the method; start executing the volume.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The EPA's A2L refrigerant transition is now in full effect for new HVAC equipment. As of January 1, 2026, all new residential and light commercial HVAC systems must use refrigerants with a GWP ≤700 — R-410A (GWP 2,088) is out for new installs. The dominant replacements are **R-454B** (GWP ~466, drop-in for many R-410A systems) and **R-32** (GWP ~675, used in ducted and ductless heat pumps). Critical for MEP designers: leak detection and repair rules have also tightened — the repair threshold for systems >1,500 lbs refrigerant dropped from 50 lbs to 15 lbs, and automatic leak detection systems are now mandatory at that size. Update your specifications and commissioning checklists accordingly if you haven't yet; ASHRAE 90.1-2022 state adoption certifications were also due by early 2026.
