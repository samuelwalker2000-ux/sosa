### 2. MY TAKE

BTC breaking below $80K on hot PPI data is a warning shot, not a trend reversal. The structural bid — IBIT pulling $335M+ in a single session as recently as May 4, total ETF AUM anchored near $62B — doesn't evaporate because of one inflation print. What changes is the timeline. Rate cuts are off the table for 2026, which means the reflexive liquidity trade (Fed pivots → BTC rips) is dead for now. The remaining bid is purely structural: ETF inflows, sovereign accumulation, and supply compression from post-halving issuance. That's durable, but it grinds rather than rockets.

The positioning play here is **patient accumulation in the $77–80K range**, not aggressive buying on the first red day. Macro headwinds are real — 10Y at 4.46%, oil above $100, a new Fed Chair to be confirmed — and crypto leveraged longs need to flush before the next leg up. Equities tell a slightly different story: rotation into industrials, energy, and materials is healthy and suggests risk appetite in the real economy is intact. Stay long quality risk (SPY, BTC spot), trim leveraged exposure, and wait for the PPI/CPI narrative to fully reprice before adding.

### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial published in *ResearchGate* confirmed that strength training improves running economy *durability* — meaning performance holds up better in the final kilometres of a long run or race compared to endurance-only training. The mechanism: heavy lower-body lifting (squats, deadlifts, single-leg work) increases neuromuscular efficiency and stiffens tendons, reducing the energy cost per stride as fatigue accumulates. Protocol recommendation: 2x/week, heavy compound lower body work 36–48 hours before a hard run session, not the day before.
