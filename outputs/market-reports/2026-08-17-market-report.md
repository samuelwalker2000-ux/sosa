### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE's updated trajectory for Standard 90.1 is now separating operational carbon from energy efficiency provisions — the new standalone "operational emissions" publication (under development) will provide an optional, cost-justified framework for reducing building GHG emissions, while the core 90.1 standard retains its role as the minimum energy efficiency baseline. The most immediate practical impact: the 2026 code cycle is mandating the **transition from HFC refrigerants to A2L mildly-flammable refrigerants** (R-32, R-454B, R-410A phase-down) across commercial HVAC and VRF systems. Designers specifying rooftop units or split systems now need to verify equipment compatibility, incorporate NFPA 1/building code A2L-specific ventilation and refrigerant-detection requirements, and confirm that mechanical rooms meet the updated safety clearances. This is not theoretical — major equipment manufacturers (Carrier, Trane, Daikin) are already shipping A2L-only product lines.
