### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial found that two strength sessions per week significantly improved running economy *durability* — meaning efficiency was maintained under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. The practical takeaway: if you're not doing heavy compound lower-body work, you're leaving performance on the table in the final miles. Prioritise Bulgarian split squats, Romanian deadlifts, and box jumps; keep loads challenging but light enough to run comfortably the next session. Source: *Strength Training Improves Running Economy Durability and Fatigued High-Intensity Performance in Well-Trained Male Runners* (ResearchGate, 2025).

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 is now in mandatory adoption territory — states were required to certify adoption by early 2026. The most impactful mechanical engineering change: buildings over 25,000 sq ft now require Energy Management Control Systems (EMCS) that monitor electrical energy by load category (HVAC, lighting, plug loads, process loads) at minimum 15-minute intervals, with 36 months of data retention. This requirement sits in Section 8 of the standard and is non-optional on new commercial projects. Engineers need to explicitly include EMCS metering points and data logging specifications in their mechanical/electrical drawings — omitting this will trigger code non-compliance flags at permit review in adopting jurisdictions.
