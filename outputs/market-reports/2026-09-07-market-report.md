### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial found that adding strength training to an endurance program improved running economy durability and produced a **35% improvement in fatigued high-intensity performance** in well-trained male runners. The key mechanism: heavier legs maintain propulsive force longer into a run, delaying form breakdown. Practical takeaway: two strength sessions per week at 50–80% 1RM targeting posterior chain (hip hinge, split squat, calf raises) is the minimum effective dose for meaningful running performance gains.
