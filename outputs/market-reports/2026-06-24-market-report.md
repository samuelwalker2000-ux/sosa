### 2. MY TAKE

This is a capitulation-flavored selloff, not a structural break. The proximate cause — a chip/AI-capex confidence wobble amplified by a hawkish BofA rate note — is sentiment-driven, and VIX jumping 15% in a session while the 10Y barely moves tells you it's a positioning unwind, not a fresh macro regime. Crypto getting dragged down in sympathy (BTC -2% on the day, -5% on the week, Fear & Greed at 23) is exactly what you'd expect when risk assets de-correlate from fundamentals and re-correlate with each other. That's not bullish, but it's also not the kind of credit-event or liquidity-crisis pattern that turns into a multi-week drawdown.

My stance here is patient, leaning toward opportunistic rather than defensive. Thursday's PCE print and Micron's earnings tonight are the two catalysts that actually matter — until those land, adding risk into a falling-VIX-fear market is just guessing the bottom. I would not chase further downside in tech-correlated alts ahead of PCE, but I'd also resist the urge to de-risk further at Extreme Fear levels with BTC dominance holding near 57% — historically this combination (broad fear, BTC outperforming alts, no credit stress) has been closer to a buyable air pocket than the start of a trend reversal. Sit on hands through Thursday, then react to the data, not the headline.
