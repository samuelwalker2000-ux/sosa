### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2026 randomized controlled trial (*ResearchGate / PubMed*) confirmed that adding heavy or explosive strength training to an endurance program improves running economy — but only when the strength sessions don't displace total running volume. The key finding: power-based movements (trap bar deadlifts, jump squats, plyometrics) performed at high velocity transferred most directly to running performance improvements. Practical application: 2x/week of 20–30 min explosive lower-body work is enough to see measurable efficiency gains without sacrificing mileage.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 NEC (National Electrical Code) has restructured load calculations under Article 130, placing Energy Management Systems (EMS) at the centre of panel and feeder sizing for commercial projects. The shift means MEP engineers must now design electrical distribution with active EMS integration in mind from schematic design — passive load schedules are no longer sufficient for code compliance on buildings adopting demand-response strategies.
