### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy lower-body strength work (e.g., 4×4 heavy back squats, 3× per week) measurably improves running economy in trained runners without adding body weight — the gain comes from increased rate of force development, not muscle hypertrophy. This means runners don't need to fear the weight room: 8 weeks of heavy compound lifting alongside endurance training will make you faster at the same aerobic effort. Prioritise squats and deadlifts at low reps (3–5) over higher-rep accessory work for this specific adaptation. *(Source: Hoff et al., 2002; Rønnestad & Mujika, 2014 — both widely cited in strength-endurance literature)*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE's Society President formally wrote to Ontario's Minister of Municipal Affairs and Housing on August 19, 2026, urging the province to incorporate **ASHRAE Standard 241 (Control of Infectious Aerosols)** and **Guideline 44-2024** into the Ontario Building Code. Toronto's Board of Health and the Ontario Society of Professional Engineers are backing the push. Standard 241, published in 2023, sets minimum ventilation and air cleaning performance targets to reduce airborne disease transmission — it's the first ASHRAE standard focused specifically on infection risk rather than just comfort/energy. For Ontario-based MEP practitioners, this is a heads-up: 241 compliance requirements could become mandatory OBC amendments within the next 1–2 code cycles. *(Source: ASHRAE President's Letter, August 2026; Toronto City Council communications, October 2026)*
