### 2. MY TAKE

This is capitulation, not a buy-the-dip blip — Fear & Greed cratering from 52 to 12 in seven days, BTC down 17% on the week, ETH sub-$1,600, and $3B+ in cumulative spot ETF outflows since May 20 all point to forced de-risking, not a healthy shakeout. The correlation breakdown is the real story: crypto is no longer trading as an inflation hedge or "digital gold," it's trading as the most liquid risk asset to sell when a hot jobs print kills rate-cut hopes and a semiconductor crash vaporizes $1T in tech value in a single session. Add an active Iran conflict pushing oil and yields higher, and you have three simultaneous shocks hitting one fragile, over-levered market.

My stance here is patient, not defensive-to-the-point-of-fleeing. Extreme Fear at 12 is historically where capitulation bottoms have formed (Dec 2018, Mar 2020, Jun 2022, Aug 2024) — but bottoms take time to confirm, and catching this knife before CPI (June 10) and FOMC (June 17) is gambling, not investing. I'd rather sit in cash/stables, let the $59K-$61K zone get tested and retested, and size back into BTC and quality majors only once ETF flows turn net-positive and the Fed signals it isn't going to lean further hawkish. This is a moment to protect capital and build a shopping list, not to be a hero.
