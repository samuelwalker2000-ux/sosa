### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2024 meta-analysis (updated interpretation confirmed in 2026 research cycles) found that adding resistance training to a running programme improves running economy by an average of **4.6%** and race performance by ~2.9% over 3–5km distances — without any change in VO2 max. The mechanism is neuromuscular: better force application per stride, not more oxygen. Heavy strength, plyometric, and isometric training all produce the effect. Practically: 2 sessions/week of compound lower-body strength work (deadlifts, single-leg press, isometric wall-sits) is the minimum effective dose for a meaningful economy gain.

*Source: NCBI — "Effect of Strength Training Methods on Middle-Distance and Long-Distance Runners' Athletic Performance," systematic review with meta-analysis (PMC11258194)*

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 62.1 compliance for VAV (Variable Air Volume) systems is getting renewed focus in the July/August 2026 edition of *Consulting-Specifying Engineer*, specifically around achieving minimum outdoor air delivery under part-load conditions. The key issue: VAV systems often violate 62.1 at low airflow set-points because the supply air ratio drops below the ventilation efficiency threshold. The fix is either DCV (Demand-Controlled Ventilation) with CO₂ sensors or a reset strategy using the Multiple Zone Ventilation Procedure — both are now effectively code-expected in new designs. Additionally, the 2026 NEC update shifts electrical load calculations to Article 130, mandating Energy Management Systems (EMS) to prevent oversized service installations on commercial projects. Worth tracking for any active project permit applications.

*Source: Consulting-Specifying Engineer, July/August 2026 issue*
