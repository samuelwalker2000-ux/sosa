### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomised controlled trial published in *Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research* confirmed that heavy strength training (≥80% 1RM, 2x/week) improves running economy durability — meaning trained runners maintain efficiency under fatigue, not just when fresh. Critically, meaningful gains appear within 8–12 weeks and continue through 16–20 weeks. If you're a runner doing only low-load gym work, shift to heavier compound lifts (Romanian deadlifts, trap bar deadlifts, single-leg press) and give it 10 weeks before judging the results.
