### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Supplementary strength training improves running economy — the oxygen cost of maintaining a given pace — which is the single most trainable aerobic performance factor for well-trained runners. A 10-week program of strength work (2x/week, focused on heavy compound lifts and plyometrics) has been shown to improve RE without requiring cuts to mileage. The key is keeping the strength sessions supplementary: no more than 20% of total training time, and programmed away from key quality run sessions to avoid accumulated fatigue.

*Source: randomised trial, Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise (2025); narrative review, Quality in Sport (July 2026)*
