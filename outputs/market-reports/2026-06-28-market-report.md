### 2. MY TAKE

BTC ripping +4% in 24 hours while the Fear & Greed Index sits at 17 — Extreme Fear — is the most interesting divergence on the board today. That's not a market shrugging off bad news on conviction; it's a bounce inside a sentiment regime that's still broken, which usually means short covering or forced buying rather than fresh capital coming in. BTC dominance holding near 59% confirms it: nobody's rotating into alts on this move, they're parking in BTC because it's the safest thing to own when sentiment is this washed out. I'd treat this as a relief bounce to fade into strength, not a trend to chase — wait for Fear & Greed to climb out of the teens before adding real risk.

Equities are sending a similar message under the hood. The S&P and NASDAQ 100 index levels look fine (+0.3%), but QQQ trading down on the day and AAPL getting hit on margin concerns tells you the move is narrower than the headline number suggests — money is leaving megacap tech, not flooding into it. Add in WTI crude collapsing 4%+ on geopolitical de-escalation and you've got a classic "indices flat, internals ugly" tape. My stance here is patient, not defensive: nothing in today's data forces a portfolio change, but I wouldn't be adding new long exposure into a market where the only thing holding the average up is breadth-light strength in a handful of names. Let the June 2 jobs report on July 2 be the next real decision point.
