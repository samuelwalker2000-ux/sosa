### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Recent research (2024–2025 RCT, published in *Journal of Strength & Conditioning Research*) confirms that strength training in the **lengthened/stretched position** — specifically slowing the eccentric (lowering) phase to 3–4 seconds and pausing at maximum stretch — produces significantly greater muscle hypertrophy and tendon remodelling compared to standard full-range lifts. For runners, this translates directly: eccentric loading of calves, hamstrings, and glutes not only builds strength but restores load tolerance in tendons, improves landing mechanics, and reduces injury risk from overuse. Priority exercise: slow-eccentric single-leg Romanian deadlifts and Nordic curls — 3x6, 4-second lowering, twice per week.
