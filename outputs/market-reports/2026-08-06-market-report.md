### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE is actively evolving its **liquid cooling standards** for data centres and high-density compute environments — but the standards update cycle is lagging behind deployment reality. MEP engineers specifying liquid cooling systems for 2026–2027 data centre projects cannot wait for published ASHRAE guidance; they must rely on manufacturer specifications, ASHRAE thermal guidelines (thermal envelopes, inlet temperature ranges) and engineering judgment. This creates liability and specification gaps on projects where liquid cooling is replacing traditional CRAC/CRAH systems. HPAC Engineering's July-August 2026 editorial theme is "Controls & Codes," covering ASHRAE, ASME, IMC, and IBC refrigerant regulation updates — worth reading for anyone doing active data centre or high-density MEP work. Proactively document the engineering basis of design thoroughly when manufacturer specs substitute for absent ASHRAE standards.
