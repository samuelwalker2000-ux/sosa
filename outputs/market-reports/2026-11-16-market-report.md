**Today's insight:** Heavy resistance training (not high-rep/low-load) combined with plyometrics is the most evidence-backed approach to improving running economy. A 2026 narrative review in *Quality in Sport* found strength training improves muscular strength, hip stability, lumbopelvic control, and running economy — with gains of 2–8% in distance runners across reviewed studies. ### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE has opened a first public review of a new standard on **product category rules (PCR) for HVAC&R-related MEP products**, with comments due December 1, 2026. This standard will define how lifecycle assessment (LCA) data is calculated and reported for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems — directly impacting Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) used in green building certifications like LEED and WELL. Additionally, ASHRAE Standard 62.1 (Ventilation and Indoor Air Quality) has an addendum in first public review (comments were due November 8), and the SSPC 62.2 committee holds a virtual interim meeting November 18. Practitioners should track the PCR standard closely — EPD requirements are increasingly appearing in Canadian OBC (Ontario Building Code) amendments and federal low-carbon procurement criteria.
