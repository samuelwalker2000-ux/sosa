### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** As of January 1, 2026, the EPA's AIM Act phasedown schedule prohibits new HVAC equipment using HFCs with GWP >750 — this kills R-410A for new installs. Compliant low-GWP replacements are R-32 (GWP 675), R-454B (GWP 466), and R-466A (GWP 733 — non-flammable). Field implication: all new mechanical specifications must reference A2L or A1 low-GWP refrigerants; existing R-410A systems can still be serviced with reclaimed refrigerant, but supply is tightening. Update your design templates and specs if you haven't.
