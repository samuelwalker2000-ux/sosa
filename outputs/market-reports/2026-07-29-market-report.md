### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** Heavy resistance training (5–8 reps at 75–85% of 1RM) is the most effective strength protocol for runners — a 2017 meta-analysis found this rep range produced the largest gains in running economy compared to lighter, higher-rep work. More importantly, newer 2024–2025 research shows the benefit is specifically to *running economy durability* — meaning trained runners maintain efficiency under fatigue, not just in fresh conditions. Two sessions of heavy compound lifts (squats, Romanian deadlifts, calf raises) per week is the minimum effective dose; results emerge within 6–10 weeks.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The EPA's 2026 refrigerant transition rules are now fully in effect: as of January 1, 2026, all industrial process cooling systems above -22°F (-30°C) must use refrigerants with a GWP ≤ 700, effectively banning R-410A (GWP 2,088) in new equipment. The primary compliant replacements are R-454B (GWP 466) and R-32 (GWP 675) — both classified as A2L (mildly flammable) under ASHRAE 34, requiring updated installation practices and ventilation design. In parallel, the EPA has tightened leak detection rules: systems containing >1,500 lbs of refrigerant must now include automatic leak detection systems, and the mandatory repair threshold drops from 50 lbs to 15 lbs of leakage — meaning MEP engineers need to spec ALD in all large commercial chiller and process cooling designs from this point forward.
