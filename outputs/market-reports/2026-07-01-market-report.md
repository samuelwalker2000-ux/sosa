### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE 90.1-2022 introduced a significant new compliance pathway — the **Total System Performance Ratio (TSPR)** in Appendix L — which evaluates an HVAC system's efficiency as an integrated whole rather than requiring each component to individually hit prescriptive minimums. This is a shift from component-level compliance to performance-based compliance, allowing engineers to trade off between, say, a slightly less efficient chiller and a more efficient cooling tower, as long as the overall system TSPR exceeds the threshold. Practically, this gives MEP engineers more design flexibility — especially on large mixed-use or institutional buildings where a single system architecture is suboptimal for prescriptive compliance but performs well holistically.
