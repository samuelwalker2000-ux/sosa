### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** State adoption of ASHRAE 90.1-2022 was required by March 6, 2026 — jurisdictions that haven't certified are now non-compliant with the federal DOE timeline. The standard's most impactful mechanical change is mandatory higher efficiency metrics for HVAC equipment: SEER2, EER2, and HSPF2 minimums are now code, pushing designers away from legacy single-stage DX systems toward variable-capacity heat pumps and VRF systems. ASHRAE has publicly committed to a carbon-neutral new construction standard by 2031 through three successive code cycles — meaning every project designed now is on a trajectory that will be code-obsolete within one or two design generations. Specify VRF and high-HSPF2 heat pump systems now to avoid redesign exposure on long-lead projects.
