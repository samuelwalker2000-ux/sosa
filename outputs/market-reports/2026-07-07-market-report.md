### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 randomized controlled trial published in ResearchGate found that strength training significantly improved running economy *durability* in well-trained male runners — meaning efficiency held up under fatigue rather than just in fresh-start conditions. The practical takeaway: don't just do strength work, prioritize the *lowering* (eccentric) phase of each rep with a 3–4 second descent and a brief pause at full stretch. This lengthened-position loading maximizes both hypertrophy stimulus and the neuromuscular transfer that makes you faster late in a run when form breaks down.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 ASHRAE Annual Conference (Austin, TX) put refrigerant transitions front and centre: sweeping EPA rules taking effect in 2026 shift requirements around leak monitoring, installation practices, and allowable refrigerant types for new equipment — HVAC contractors and MEP engineers designing new systems or specifying replacements must verify compliance now, not at permit submission.
