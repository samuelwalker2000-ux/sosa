### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** The 2026 edition of NFPA 855 (Standard for the Installation of Stationary Energy Storage Systems) significantly expands requirements for battery-based ESS installations in commercial and mixed-use buildings. The new edition introduces mandatory formal hazard analyses (similar to a Process Hazard Analysis) for systems exceeding specific capacity thresholds, strengthens emergency response planning and training requirements, and tightens separation distances between ESS units and occupied spaces. For MEP engineers specifying battery backup or grid-tied ESS systems on commercial projects, the 2026 NFPA 855 will now require documented hazard analysis submissions alongside permit applications in jurisdictions that have adopted the new edition — a process that should be scoped into project timelines early.

*Source: NFPA 855 (2026 Edition); Summit Fire Protection / HFM Magazine reporting*
