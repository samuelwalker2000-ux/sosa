### 9. FITNESS

**Today's insight:** A 2025 RCT published in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* (Zanini, Folland, Wu & Blagrove, Loughborough University) found that a 10-week supplementary strength program preserved running economy over 90 minutes at high intensity in well-trained male runners — suggesting heavy compound lifts don't just build muscle, they protect performance when fatigued. Practically: two strength sessions per week with squats, deadlifts, and single-leg exercises alongside your run training appears sufficient to yield the benefit. The key is progressive overload over at least 8-10 weeks — shorter blocks don't produce the neuromuscular adaptations that actually shift running economy.

### 10. MEP ENGINEERING

**Today's technical item:** ASHRAE submitted comments to the California Energy Commission in July 2026 recommending that the 2028 California Energy Code reference the 2025 edition of Standard 90.1 — the primary energy efficiency standard for commercial buildings. The 2025 edition tightens envelope performance requirements and updates minimum efficiency thresholds for HVAC and lighting systems, making it more stringent than the 2022 edition currently used in most jurisdictions. For MEP engineers working on projects in California or jurisdictions that model-code after ASHRAE 90.1, it's worth reviewing the 2025 edition now — particularly the envelope trade-off compliance path and the updated fan system requirements — since the 2028 adoption cycle will likely set the standard for the next decade of commercial design.
